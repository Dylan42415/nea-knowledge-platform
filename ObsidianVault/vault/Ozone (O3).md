# Ozone (O3)


## Summary
Ground-level ozone (O3) is a secondary pollutant formed in the troposphere via complex photochemical reactions involving NOX and VOCs in the presence of sunlight. Singapore has consistently failed to meet the WHO AQG (2005) 8-hour maximum mean limit of 100 µg/m³, and levels increased by 12.0% during the 2020 COVID-19 Circuit Breaker due to atmospheric photochemical dynamics and reduced NOX titration.
