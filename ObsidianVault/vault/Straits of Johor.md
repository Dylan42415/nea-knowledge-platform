# Straits of Johor


## Summary
The Straits of Johor (SOJ) is divided into SOJ East and SOJ West monitoring sectors for coastal water quality assessment. Monthly mean Enterococcus levels along SOJ fluctuated between 2018 and 2020 due to land-based run-offs, sewer leakages, shipping discharges, and tidal currents.

## Key Data / Findings

| Sub-region | Evaluated Indicators | Guideline Limits |
|---|---|---|
| SOJ East | Dissolved Oxygen, Enterococcus | DO ≥ 4 mg/l, Enterococcus ≤ 200 counts/100ml |
| SOJ West | Dissolved Oxygen, Enterococcus | DO ≥ 4 mg/l, Enterococcus ≤ 200 counts/100ml |
