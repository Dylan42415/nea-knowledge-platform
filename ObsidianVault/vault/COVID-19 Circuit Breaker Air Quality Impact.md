# COVID-19 Circuit Breaker Air Quality Impact


## Summary
Singapore implemented a two-month Circuit Breaker (CB) from 7th April to 1st June 2020 to curb COVID-19 transmission. The resulting restrictions significantly decreased human mobility and vehicular traffic, leading to widespread reductions in primary air pollutants but a +12.0% increase in secondary ground-level ozone.

## Key Data / Findings

| Air Quality Parameter | Change during Circuit Breaker | Attribution / Primary Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fine Particulate Matter (PM2.5) | -28.8% | Reduced road mobility / traffic |
| Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2) | -38.1% | Reduced vehicular emissions |
| Sulfur Dioxide (SO2) | -58.1% | Industrial, refinery & shipping slowdown |
| Carbon Monoxide (CO) | -5.6% | Traffic and combustion reduction |
| Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) | -21.4% | Composite air quality improvement |
| Ground-level Ozone (O3) | +12.0% | Photochemical reaction & higher VOC/NOX ratio |
