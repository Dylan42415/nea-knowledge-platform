# Emerging Contaminants (ECs)


## Summary
Emerging contaminants (ECs) are synthetic or naturally derived compounds that pose potential or known risks to aquatic ecosystems and human health. They occur in urban surface waters at low concentrations ranging from ng/L to μg/L, entering via wastewater treatment outflow, leaky sewers, landfills, and runoff.
