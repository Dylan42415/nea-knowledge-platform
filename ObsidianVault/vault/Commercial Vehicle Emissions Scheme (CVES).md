# Commercial Vehicle Emissions Scheme (CVES)


## Summary
A scheme introduced on 1 April 2021 for light commercial vehicles mirroring the VES to promote the adoption of cleaner commercial fleets.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Introduction Date | 1 April 2021 |
| Target Fleet | Light commercial vehicles |
