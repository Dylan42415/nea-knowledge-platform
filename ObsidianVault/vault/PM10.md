# PM10


## Summary
PM10 refers to particulate matter of size 10µm or below in diameter that can be inhaled and deposited throughout the airways and lungs, with 2020 annual mean levels exceeding WHO guidelines but hitting a 10-year low.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Standard | Value |
|---|---|
| PM10 WHO AQG (Annual Mean) | 20 µg/m³ |
| 2020 Performance | Exceeded WHO AQG, lowest over last 10 years |
