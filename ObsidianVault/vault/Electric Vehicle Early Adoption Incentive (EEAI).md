# Electric Vehicle Early Adoption Incentive (EEAI)


## Summary
A tax rebate introduced on 1 January 2021 on the purchase of new electric cars or taxis to promote a cleaner passenger vehicle fleet.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Introduction Date | 1 January 2021 |
| Application | Purchase of a new electric car or taxi |
