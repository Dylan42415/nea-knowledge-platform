# Initial IMO Strategy on Reduction of GHG Emissions from Ships


## Summary
Adopted in 2018 by the International Maritime Organisation (IMO), the Initial IMO Strategy aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from international shipping by at least 50% by 2050 compared to 2008 levels, while pursuing efforts to phase them out entirely.
