# PFOA and PFOS


## Summary
PFOA and PFOS are legacy long-chain hydrophobic PFAS compounds. In Singapore surface water surveys, concentrations were detected at <21 ng/L for PFOA and <24 ng/L for PFOS, both falling safely below USEPA drinking water health advisory limits.

## Key Data / Findings

## Key Data / Findings

| Chemical | Detected Concentration (Singapore) | USEPA Health Advisory Standard |
|---|---|---|
| PFOS | <24 ng/L | 70 ng/L (Combined PFOA + PFOS) |
| PFOA | <21 ng/L | 70 ng/L (Combined PFOA + PFOS) |
