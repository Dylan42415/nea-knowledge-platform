# ESBL resistant Escherichia coli


## Summary
Extended-spectrum β-lactamases (ESBL) resistant Escherichia coli is a multidrug-resistant bacterium monitored in Singapore's marine and aquaculture waters to track antimicrobial resistance.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Average Concentration | Benchmark / Comparison | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Singapore Coastal & Aquaculture Sites | 10 CFU/100mL | High compared to European baselines | Ng et al. 2018 |
| Netherlands Recreational Waters (2010) | 1 CFU/100mL | Benchmark for coastal waters | Schijven et al. 2015 |
| Other Observed Locations | Present | Coastal and recreational waters | Brazil, Norway, India |
