# Dissolved Oxygen (DO)


## Summary
Dissolved Oxygen (DO) measures the amount of oxygen dissolved in water required for aquatic organisms' respiration and ecological health. Coastal water quality guidelines specify a threshold of ≥4 mg/l under AMWQC, while inland water guidelines specify 5 mg/l for Class I rivers and ≥3 mg/l for Class IV rivers and non-catchment waters.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Type / Class | Benchmark Guideline Value | Standard Source |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal Waters | ≥ 4 mg/l | ASEAN Marine Water Quality Criteria (AMWQC) |
| Class I Inland Rivers | 5 mg/l | ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action (2005) |
| Class IV Inland Rivers / Non-catchment | ≥ 3 mg/l | ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action (2005) |
