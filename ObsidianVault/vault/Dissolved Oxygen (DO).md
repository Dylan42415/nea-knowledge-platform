# Dissolved Oxygen (DO)


## Summary
Dissolved oxygen (DO) measures the amount of oxygen available in water to support aquatic life respiration. In 2020, DO levels in coastal waters met the 4 mg/l guideline across all months, and long-term 10-year annual means remained stable.

## Key Data / Findings

| Category / Standard | Threshold Guideline | 2020 Performance / Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coastal Waters | ≥ 4 mg/l | Met for all months of 2020 | AMWQC aquatic life protection standard |
| Inland Waters (Class I) | 5 mg/l | Reference standard | 2005 ASEAN Strategic Plan |
| Inland Waters (Class IV) | 3 mg/l | Reference standard | 2005 ASEAN Strategic Plan |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≥ 3 mg/l | Maintained above guideline (2011–2020) | Fig 8 annual mean trend |
