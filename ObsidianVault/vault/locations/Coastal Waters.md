# Coastal Waters


## Summary
Singapore's coastal waters are monitored along Straits of Johor (SOJ) West, SOJ East, and Straits of Singapore (SOS). In 2020, Dissolved Oxygen (DO) met the ≥4 mg/l guideline for all months, whereas Enterococcus breached the 200 counts/100ml limit in May 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Guideline Value | 2020 Performance | Long-Term Trend (2011–2020) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dissolved Oxygen (DO) | ≥ 4 mg/l | Met guidelines all months of 2020 | Annual mean levels remained relatively stable |
| Enterococcus | ≤ 200 counts/100 ml | Did not meet guideline in May 2020 | Annual mean levels remained relatively stable |
