# Carbon Monoxide (CO)


## Summary
Carbon Monoxide is formed from incomplete combustion and acts as an ozone precursor; Singapore's maximum 8-hour mean in 2020 was 1.2 mg/m³, well within the WHO guideline of 10 mg/m³.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Standard | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Max 8-hour Mean | 1.2 mg/m³ | Lowest recorded concentration over last 10 years |
| WHO AQG (2005) | 10 mg/m³ | Target threshold |
| Industrial CO Standard (New) | 250 mg/Nm³ | Tightened in 2015 (by 2023 for existing, down from 625 mg/Nm³) |
