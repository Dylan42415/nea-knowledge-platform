# Artificial Sweetener Chemical Markers


## Summary
Artificial sweeteners, including acesulfame, cyclamate, and saccharin, serve as chemical markers to detect and differentiate diffuse sewage leakage in urban surface waters from illicit discharges or surface runoff.

## Key Data / Findings

## Key Data / Findings

| Indicator Compounds | Application / Target | Screening Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Acesulfame, Cyclamate, Saccharin | Marker for sewage leakage in surface waters | High detection frequency, low attenuation, high lability/conservativeness ratio |
