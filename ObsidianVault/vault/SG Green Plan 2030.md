# SG Green Plan 2030


## Summary
A national sustainability blueprint detailing infrastructure expansions such as growing the rail and cycling networks to reduce transport emissions.

## Key Data / Findings

| Infrastructure Metric | 2020/2021 Baseline | 2030 Target |
|---|---|---|
| Rail Network | Around 230 km (2021) | 360 km by early 2030s |
| Cycling Network | 460 km (2020) | 1,320 km by 2030 |

## Relationships
- **MANAGED_BY** → [[National Environment Agency]]

## Source Excerpt
> As part of the SG Green Plan 2030, the rail network will be expanded from around 230km in 2021 to 360km by the early 2030s, and the cycling network will be expanded from 460km in 2020 to 1,320km by 2030.
 — soe_report.pdf, pp. 29-32
