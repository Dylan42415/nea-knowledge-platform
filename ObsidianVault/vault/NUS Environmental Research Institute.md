# NUS Environmental Research Institute


## Summary
NUS Environmental Research Institute (NERI) is a research institute at the National University of Singapore. NERI partnered with NEA in a five-year study (2015–2020) led by Associate Professor Yu Liya to characterize PM2.5 properties and develop the peat forest smoke indicator.
