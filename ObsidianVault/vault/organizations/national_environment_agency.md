# National Environment Agency


## Summary
The National Environment Agency (NEA) is Singapore's leading public organization for environmental protection. NEA conducts field trials of low-cost ambient air quality sensor networks and partners with academic institutions to investigate particulate pollution.

## Key Data / Findings

| Program / Trial Name | Parameter / Scale | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Low-Cost Sensor Residential Trial | 28 Sensors deployed on lampposts/bridges | Six-month trial |
| Low-Cost Sensor Reliability Study | Performance under local conditions | 2017/2018 |
| NUS/NERI Research Collaboration | Characterization of PM2.5 in Singapore | 5-year period (2015 to 2020) |

## Source Excerpt
> NEA has been conducting field trials of low-cost sensors capable of measuring both fine particulate matter (PM2.5) and nitrogen dioxide (NO2)... From 2015 to 2020, NEA embarked on a collaboration with NUS Environmental Research Institute (NERI) to study the characterization of PM2.5 in Singapore.
