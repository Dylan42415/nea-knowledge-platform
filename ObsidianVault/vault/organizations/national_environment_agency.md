# National Environment Agency


## Summary
The National Environment Agency (NEA) is Singapore's primary statutory board responsible for air quality monitoring, establishing industrial emission standards, and regulating pollutant content in transport fuels.

## Key Data / Findings

| Regulation / Mandate | Implemented Value / Standard | Target Pollutant |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial Emission Limit | 5 mg/Nm³ | Benzene |
| Transport Fuel Content Limit | 1% by volume (implemented 2017) | Benzene |
| Air Quality Target Benchmark | WHO AQG (2005) Standards | Ambient Pollutants |
| Treaty Compliance | Signatory since 2001 | Stockholm Convention (POPs) |
