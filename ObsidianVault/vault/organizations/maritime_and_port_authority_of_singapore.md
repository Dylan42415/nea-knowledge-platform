# Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore


## Summary
The Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) is the statboard responsible for maritime development in Singapore, currently developing the Maritime Singapore Decarbonisation Blueprint 2050 to reduce shipping emissions.
