# Centre for Remote Imaging, Sensing and Processing (CRISP)


## Summary
CRISP, based at the National University of Singapore (NUS), develops remote sensing technologies, satellite image processing applications, and numerical meteorological models (such as WRF-Chem) to study smoke haze transport in Southeast Asia.

## Key Data / Findings

| Research Focus | Tools / Models |
|---|---|
| Satellite Remote Sensing | MODIS (Terra/Aqua), Himawari-8/9, GEMS |
| Numerical Modeling | WRF-Chem (V3.6) customized for Singapore region |
| Atmospheric Profiling | AERONET Sun-photometer, MPLNET LIDAR |
