# Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA)


## Summary
The Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) leads Singapore's maritime sector decarbonisation efforts in partnership with industry stakeholders. It is developing the Maritime Singapore Decarbonisation Blueprint 2050 to transition domestic vessels and port infrastructure away from diesel and bunker fuel.

## Key Data / Findings

| Policy Instrument | Scope | Key Focus Areas |
|---|---|---|
| Maritime Singapore Decarbonisation Blueprint 2050 | Domestic harbour craft, Singapore-flagged ships, port equipment | Electrification, transition to low-carbon or zero-carbon fuels |
