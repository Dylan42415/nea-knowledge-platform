# National Environment Agency


## Summary
The National Environment Agency (NEA) is the regulatory authority in Singapore responsible for environmental monitoring, including ambient air quality, inland waters, coastal waters, and recreational beach water quality.

## Key Data / Findings

| Responsible Domain | Monitored Water Bodies / Locations | Standards Applied |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal Waters Monitoring | SOJ East, SOJ West, SOS | AMWQC, WHO Guidelines |
| Recreational Beaches | 7 Monitored Beaches | WHO Recreational Guidelines |
| Inland Waters Monitoring | Catchment & Non-catchment Waters | ASEAN Strategic Plan (2005) |
