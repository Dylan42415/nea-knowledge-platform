# National Environment Agency


## Summary
The National Environment Agency (NEA) oversees environmental quality management in Singapore. Its responsibilities include reviewing industrial air emission limits, monitoring incineration plant emissions, managing water quality across inland and coastal waters, providing weekly BSWI beach advisories, and tracking emerging international pollutants.

## Key Data / Findings

| Domain | Regulatory & Monitoring Actions |
|---|---|
| Air Quality | In-stack emission monitoring for incineration plants, industrial limit reviews |
| Water Bodies Monitored | Inland water bodies, recreational beaches (7 sites), coastal waters |
| Public Advisories | Beach Short-term Water Quality Information (BSWI, launched end of 2020) |
| Emerging Contaminants | Marine microplastics, marine litter |
