# Centre for Remote Imaging, Sensing and Processing (CRISP)


## Summary
CRISP at NUS conducts research on remote sensing technologies, vertical profiling, satellite imaging, and numerical modeling (WRF-Chem) for haze tracking.

## Key Data / Findings

| Research Area | Tools / Models |
|---|---|
| Atmospheric Profiling | AERONET Sun-photometer, MPLNET LIDAR |
| Numerical Modeling | WRF-Chem (customized V3.6 model over 9x9 km² grid) |
