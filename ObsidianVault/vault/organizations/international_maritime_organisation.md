# International Maritime Organisation


## Summary
The International Maritime Organisation (IMO) is the UN specialized agency responsible for regulating shipping. In 2018, it adopted the Initial IMO Strategy to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from international shipping by at least 50% by 2050 compared to 2008.

## Key Data / Findings

| Policy / Strategy | Adoption Year | Primary Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Initial IMO Strategy | 2018 | Cut international shipping GHG emissions by ≥50% by 2050 vs 2008 |
