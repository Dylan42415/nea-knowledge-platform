# Environmental Monitoring & Modelling Division (EMMD)


## Summary
The Environmental Monitoring and Modelling Division (EMMD) is a division under the National Environment Agency (NEA) that monitors, models, and assesses the quality of Singapore’s ambient air and water bodies.

## Key Data / Findings

| Monitoring Scope | Key Activities |
|---|---|
| Environmental Mediums | Ambient air, water bodies |
| Core Functions | Upstream planning & control, surveillance, enforcement, downstream monitoring, modelling, research, policy review |
