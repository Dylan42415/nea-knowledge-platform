# National Environment Agency (NEA)


## Summary
The National Environment Agency (NEA) is the statutory board responsible for monitoring air quality, publishing pollutant concentrations, tightening emission and fuel standards, and enforcing pollution control measures in Singapore.

## Key Data / Findings

| Action / Initiative | Details |
|---|---|
| 1-hour PM2.5 Publishing | Published across five regions since 2014; bandings introduced in 2016 |
| Vehicle Emission Standards | Adopted Euro VI for petrol vehicles from 1 Sept 2017; introduced Vehicular Emission Scheme (VES) in 2018 |
| Industrial Emission Standards | Tightened standards for SO2, NO2, and CO for new plants in 2015 and existing plants by 2023 |
| Motorcycle Deregistration Incentive Scheme | Provides incentives for early deregistration of older high-emission motorcycles |
