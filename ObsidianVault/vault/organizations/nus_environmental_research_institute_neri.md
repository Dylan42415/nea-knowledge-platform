# NUS Environmental Research Institute (NERI)


## Summary
The NUS Environmental Research Institute (NERI) is an academic research department within the National University of Singapore. NERI partnered with NEA to conduct high-tech physical and chemical characterization of PM2.5 in Singapore from 2015 to 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Program | Collaboration Duration | Target Parameters |
|---|---|---|
| PM2.5 Characterization Project | 5 years (2015 to 2020) | Size, weight, number concentration, and chemical composition of PM2.5 |
