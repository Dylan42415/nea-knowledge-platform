# NUS Environmental Research Institute (NERI)


## Summary
NUS Environmental Research Institute (NERI) is an academic research body that collaborated with NEA from 2015 to 2020 to characterize PM2.5 and identify peatland forest smoke sources.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Collaboration Duration | 5-year partnership (2015 to 2020) |
| Primary Outputs | Development of the Peat Forest Smoke Indicator; chemical composition assays of local PM2.5 |
