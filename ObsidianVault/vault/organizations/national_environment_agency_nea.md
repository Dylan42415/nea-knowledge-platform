# National Environment Agency (NEA)


## Summary
The National Environment Agency (NEA) is the statutory board under which the Environmental Monitoring and Modelling Division (EMMD) operates to safeguard Singapore's clean environment.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Parent Group | Clean Environment Group | National environmental governance |
| Core Functions | Upstream development planning, surveillance, enforcement, downstream monitoring, modelling, research | National Environment Management |

### Additional Findings (National Environment Agency (NEA))

| Function / Action | Details |
|---|---|
| PM2.5 Monitoring | Publishing 1-hour PM2.5 concentrations across five regions since 2014 |
| Vehicular Standards | Adopted Euro VI emission standards from 1 Sept 2017; introduced Vehicular Emission Scheme (VES) in 2018 |
| Industrial Standards | Tightened industrial standards for SO2, NO2, and CO for new and existing plants (2015/2023) |
| Incentives | Motorcycle Deregistration Incentive Scheme for older motorcycles |
