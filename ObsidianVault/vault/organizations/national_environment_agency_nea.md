# National Environment Agency (NEA)


## Summary
The National Environment Agency monitors inland, coastal, and recreational water quality in Singapore through manual sampling, continuous sensors, and pollution control programmes.

## Key Data / Findings

| Operational Domain | Monitoring Scope |
|---|---|
| Inland Waters | 74 sampling locations across catchments and non-catchments |
| Coastal Waters | 43 sampling points and 8 real-time buoy stations |
| Recreational Beaches | 7 popular beaches managed via BSWI and weekly Enterococcus testing |
