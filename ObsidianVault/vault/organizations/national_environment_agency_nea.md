# National Environment Agency (NEA)


## Summary
The National Environment Agency (NEA) is the public organization responsible for safeguarding Singapore's clean environment through planning, development control, surveillance, enforcement, environmental monitoring, modelling, and policy reviews.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Target / Benchmark Reference | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Core Target | WHO Air Quality Guidelines (WHO AQG) | Long-term target to achieve WHO AQG (2005) |
| Sampling Programs | Manual Air Sampling | Fortnightly (Lead) & Annual (Dioxins) |

## Source Excerpt
> As part of the National Environment Agency (NEA)’s Clean Environment Group, EMMD plays an integral role in safeguarding our clean environment. This includes upstream development planning and control, surveillance and enforcement, downstream environmental monitoring, modelling, and research, as well as review of policies for pollution management.
