# National Environment Agency (NEA)


## Summary
The National Environment Agency (NEA) monitors the water quality of inland water bodies, coastal areas, and recreational beaches using continuous monitoring sensors and manual sampling programmes.

## Key Data / Findings

| Monitoring Domain | Scope / Network Size |
|---|---|
| Inland Waters | 74 sampling locations (catchment and non-catchment areas) |
| Coastal Waters | 43 sampling points along Straits of Singapore and Straits of Johor; 8 buoy-based stations |
| Recreational Beaches | 7 popular beaches |
