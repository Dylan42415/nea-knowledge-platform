# National Environment Agency (NEA)


## Summary
The National Environment Agency monitors inland water bodies, coastal areas, and recreational beaches through online sensors, manual sampling, and continuous monitoring networks.

## Key Data / Findings

| Monitoring Program | Scope / Scale |
|---|---|
| Inland Water Monitoring | 74 sampling locations in catchment and non-catchment areas |
| Coastal Water Monitoring | 43 sampling points along Straits of Singapore and Straits of Johor |
| Continuous Coastal Monitoring | 8 buoy-based monitoring stations commissioned in 2014 |
| Recreational Beaches | 7 popular beaches monitored for Enterococcus |
