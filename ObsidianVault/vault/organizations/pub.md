# PUB


## Summary
PUB is Singapore's national water agency responsible for monitoring the water quality of reservoirs. Reservoirs form part of the local catchment, which represents one of Singapore's four National Taps for potable drinking water.

## Key Data / Findings

| Functional Domain | Domain Scope |
|---|---|
| Monitoring Focus | Reservoir water quality |
| Output Role | Potable drinking water supply (National Taps) |
