# Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore


## Summary
The Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) is the agency leading the development of the Maritime Singapore Decarbonisation Blueprint 2050 in partnership with industry stakeholders to reduce maritime emissions.
