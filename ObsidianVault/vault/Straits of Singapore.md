# Straits of Singapore


## Summary
The Straits of Singapore (SOS) represents a primary coastal monitoring region. Dissolved Oxygen levels in SOS consistently satisfied the ≥4 mg/l coastal water standard throughout 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Zone Name | Monitored Parameters | Performance Target |
|---|---|---|
| Straits of Singapore (SOS) | Dissolved Oxygen (DO), Enterococcus | DO ≥ 4 mg/l, Enterococcus ≤ 200 counts/100ml |
