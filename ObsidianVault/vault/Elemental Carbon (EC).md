# Elemental Carbon (EC)


## Summary
Elemental carbon (EC) is a primary component of carbonaceous compounds found in smoke-laden PM2.5. It is categorized into char-EC and soot-EC, whose relative proportions indicate the combustion conditions and source of transported biomass smoke.

## Key Data / Findings

| Subtype Category | Combustion Indication | Primary Emissions Associated |
|---|---|---|
| Char-like EC | Smoldering-prominent / Underground peat burning | Organic gases and particulates |
| Soot-like EC | Flaming-prominent / Ground-level forest fires | Inorganics (sulfates, heavy metals) |

> Elemental carbon (EC) found in smoke laden PM2.5 can be categorised into two types – char and soot.
