# National Environment Agency (NEA)


## Summary
The National Environment Agency (NEA) is the statutory board responsible for monitoring air quality, publishing pollutant concentrations, tightening emission standards, and implementing vehicular and industrial schemes in Singapore.

## Key Data / Findings

| Function / Responsibility | Action / Instrument |
|---|---|
| Air Quality Monitoring | Publishes hourly 1-hour PM2.5 and PSI indicators |
| Standards Enforcement | Tightened industrial SO2, NO2, and CO standards |
| Vehicular Schemes | Adopted Euro VI (2017), Vehicular Emission Scheme (2018), Motorcycle Deregistration Incentive Scheme |
