# Particulate Matter (PM10)


## Summary
PM10 refers to particulate matter of size 10µm or below in diameter that can be inhaled and deposited throughout the airways and lungs, with 2020 annual levels exceeding WHO guidelines but hitting a 10-year low.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Standard | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Annual Mean Level | Lowest over last 10 years | Exceeds WHO AQG (2005) |
| PM10 WHO AQG (Annual Mean) | 20 µg/m³ | WHO Air Quality Guideline |
| Major Sources | Industries and motor vehicles | Local & Regional emissions |
