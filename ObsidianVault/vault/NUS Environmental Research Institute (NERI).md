# NUS Environmental Research Institute (NERI)


## Summary
The NUS Environmental Research Institute (NERI) is a research entity at the National University of Singapore. NERI collaborated with NEA to research the physical and chemical composition of PM2.5 in Singapore, developing advanced indicators to trace regional pollution sources.

## Key Data / Findings

| Research Collaboration Scope | Key Research Deliverables | Timeline | Source Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| PM2.5 Characterization Project | Analyzed PM2.5 physical properties (size, weight, concentration) & chemical compositions | 2015 - 2020 | p. 44 |
| Peat Forest Smoke Indicator | Tracked regional haze and peatland burning markers | Utilized in 2019 haze episode | p. 44 |
