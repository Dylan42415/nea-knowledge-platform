# ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action on Water Resources Management Standards


## Summary
Adopted in 2005, the ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action on Water Resources Management provides guideline values for Class I and Class IV rivers to evaluate inland water quality. Class I standards are stringent to ensure suitability for potable water use.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Class I Guideline Standard | Class IV Guideline Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Dissolved oxygen | 5 mg/l | 3 mg/l |
| Total suspended solids | 50 mg/l | 300 mg/l |
| Biochemical oxygen demand | 5 mg/l | 10 mg/l |
