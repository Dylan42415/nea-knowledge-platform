# Laser Light Scattering Technology


## Summary
Laser light scattering technology measures PM2.5 by directing laser beams through air samples, causing large particles to scatter light at smaller angles and small particles to scatter light at larger angles. Intensity variations across scattering angles determine particle sizes.

## Key Data / Findings

| Component / Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| Monitored Pollutant | PM2.5 (fine particulate matter) |
| Scattering Principle | Large particles = smaller angles; Small particles = larger angles |
| Core Hardware Modules | Laser Light Source, Sample Module, Detector, Light Trap, Average Intensity Analyzer |
