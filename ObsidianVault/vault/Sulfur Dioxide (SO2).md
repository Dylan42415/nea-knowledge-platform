# Sulfur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
Sulfur dioxide (SO2) is an industrial pollutant emitted mainly by energy generation, petroleum refining, and shipping activities. Despite a 58.1% reduction in 24-hour SO2 levels during the 2020 Circuit Breaker period, Singapore did not meet the WHO AQG (2005) guidelines for SO2 in 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Parameter | Value / Status | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Circuit Breaker SO2 Reduction | -58.1% | Relative to 2017–2019 baseline |
| 2020 Compliance Status | Exceeded WHO AQG (2005) | WHO AQG (2005) Target |
| Major Emission Sources | Industries, Refineries, Shipping | Industrial activities |
