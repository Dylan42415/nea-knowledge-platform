# Beach Short-term Water Quality Information (BSWI)


## Summary
BSWI is a public water quality information system launched by NEA at the end of 2020. It provides weekly assessments, advisories, and recommended precautions for Singapore's seven popular recreational beaches.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value / Frequency |
|---|---|
| Implementation Date | End of 2020 |
| Update Frequency | Weekly |
| Geographical Scope | 7 popular recreational beaches in Singapore |
| Managing Agency | National Environment Agency (NEA) |
