# Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2)


## Summary
Nitrogen Dioxide is emitted from industrial fuel combustion and vehicle engines, with Singapore meeting the WHO annual mean guideline of 40 µg/m³ by recording a 10-year low annual mean of 20 µg/m³ in 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Standard | Value | Target / Guideline |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Annual NO2 Mean | 20 µg/m³ | WHO AQG (2005): 40 µg/m³ (Meeting guideline) |
| Industrial NO2 Standard (New Plants) | 400 mg/Nm³ | Tightened in 2015 from 700 mg/Nm³ |
| Industrial NO2 Standard (Existing Plants) | 400 mg/Nm³ | Target by 2023 |
