# Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2)


## Summary
Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2) is emitted from fuel combustion in industries and vehicle engines; Singapore met the WHO AQG of 40 µg/m³ with a 2020 annual mean of 20 µg/m³, the lowest in a decade.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Standard | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Annual Mean | 20 µg/m³ | Lowest in last 10 years |
| WHO AQG (2005) | 40 µg/m³ | Met consistently over past decade |
| Industrial NO2 Standard (New) | 400 mg/Nm³ | Tightened in 2015 (by 2023 for existing, down from 700 mg/Nm³) |
