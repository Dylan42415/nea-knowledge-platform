# SO2


## Summary
Sulfur dioxide (SO2) is a gaseous air pollutant monitored in Singapore's ambient air and via spaceborne instruments such as GEMS onboard GEO-KOMPSAT 2B.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Standard / Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ambient Target | Working towards WHO final Air Quality Guidelines (2005) | NEA |
| Satellite Detection | GEMS (UV-visible spectral range) | GEO-KOMPSAT 2B |
