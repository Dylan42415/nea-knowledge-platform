# Aerosol Optical Depth (AOD)


## Summary
Aerosol Optical Depth (AOD) is a standard spectroscopic measurement that gauges the total concentration of aerosol or smoke particles from the ground to the top of the atmosphere, serving as a proxy for ground-level PM2.5.

## Key Data / Findings

| Measuring Platform | Instrumentation Type | Utility |
|---|---|---|
| Ground-based Atmospheric Profiling | AERONET Sun-photometer | Total concentration at multiple spectral bands |
| Environmental Satellites | MODIS, Himawari8/9, GEMS | Proxy for ground level PM2.5 concentrations |
