# Aerosol Optical Depth (AOD)


## Summary
A standard data product generated by environmental satellites used as a proxy for ground-level particle concentrations.

## Relationships
- **USED_AS_PROXY_FOR** → [[PM2.5]]

## Source Excerpt
> AOD is also a standard data product generated by environmental satellites and is typically used as a proxy for particle concentration measured by standard PM2.5 samplers at the ground level.
