# Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)


## Summary
Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) measures the oxygen consumed by organic matter in water bodies. In 2020, BOD levels met NEA guidelines across catchment and non-catchment waters except in December 2020 due to intense rainfall washdown.

## Key Data / Findings

| Environment | Guideline Threshold | 2020 Performance Exception | 10-Year Long-Term Performance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≤5 mg/l | Non-compliant in Dec 2020 (intense rainfall washdown) | Maintained within guideline over last 10 years |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≤10 mg/l | Non-compliant in Dec 2020 (intense rainfall washdown) | Maintained within guideline over last 10 years |
