# Particulate Matter (PM10 and PM2.5)


## Summary
Particulate matter includes solid and liquid particles categorized by size (PM10 and PM2.5), which cause respiratory and cardiovascular problems at elevated levels.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---||
| PM10 Annual Mean WHO AQG | 20 µg/m³ | WHO AQG (2005) |
| PM2.5 Annual Mean WHO AQG | 10 µg/m³ | WHO AQG (2005) |
