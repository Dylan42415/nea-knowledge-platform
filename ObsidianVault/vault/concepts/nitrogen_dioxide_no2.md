# Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2)


## Summary
An air pollutant emitted from fuel combustion in industries and vehicles, with annual mean levels subject to WHO Air Quality Guidelines.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2020 Annual Mean | 20 µg/m3 |
| WHO AQG (2005) | 40 µg/m3 |
| Industrial NO2 (New) | 400 mg/Nm3 (from 700 mg/Nm3) |
| Industrial NO2 (Existing) | 400 mg/Nm3 (by 2023) |
