# Dissolved Oxygen (DO)


## Summary
Dissolved Oxygen (DO) measures the amount of oxygen dissolved in water, required by aquatic organisms for respiration and serving as a key indicator of ecological health. In 2020, coastal waters met the DO guideline of 4 mg/l across all months.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Domain | Value / Guideline | Benchmark Standard | 2020 Performance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coastal Waters | ≥ 4 mg/l | ASEAN Marine Water Quality Criteria (AMWQC) | Met guideline for all months of 2020 |
| Inland Waters (Class I) | 5 mg/l | ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action (2005) | N/A |
| Inland Waters (Class IV) | ≥ 3 mg/l | ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action (2005) | N/A |
