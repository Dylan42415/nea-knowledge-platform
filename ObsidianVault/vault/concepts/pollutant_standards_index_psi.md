# Pollutant Standards Index (PSI)


## Summary
The Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) is Singapore's primary index utilized to report and benchmark general ambient air quality performance.

## Key Data / Findings

| Reporting Index | Associated Parameters |
|---|---|
| Pollutant Standards Index | 6 criteria air pollutants (SO2, NO2, O3, CO, PM10, PM2.5) |
