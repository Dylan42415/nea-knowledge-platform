# Electrochemical Cell Technology


## Summary
A sensor detection method that measures target gas concentrations (such as NO2) by registering the electrical current generated when the gas reacts with internal sensing chemicals.

## Relationships
- **UTILIZED_BY** → [[Low-Cost Air Quality Sensors]]

## Source Excerpt
> The measurement of NO2 is carried out via electrochemical cell technology which uses chemical reactions to generate a current when NO2 gas comes into contact with chemicals within the sensor.
