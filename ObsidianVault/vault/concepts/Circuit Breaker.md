# Circuit Breaker


## Summary
The Circuit Breaker (CB) was a two-month COVID-19 pandemic containment measure implemented in Singapore from 7 April to 1 June 2020. The restriction on movement reduced primary outdoor air pollutants substantially while inducing a counter-intuitive 12.0% increase in ground-level ozone.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Indicator | CB Period Change (7 Apr – 1 Jun 2020) | Baseline Period (2017–2019) |
|---|---|---|
| Fine Particulate Matter (PM2.5) | -28.8% | Same-period average |
| Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2) | -38.1% | Same-period average |
| Carbon Monoxide (CO) | -5.6% | Same-period average |
| Sulfur Dioxide (SO2) | -58.1% | Same-period average |
| Ground-level Ozone (O3) | +12.0% | Same-period average |
| Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) | -21.4% | Same-period average |
