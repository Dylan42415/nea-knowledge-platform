# COVID-19 Circuit Breaker


## Summary
A period of movement restrictions in 2020 used as a unique research opportunity to study the impact of reduced economic activity on urban air and water quality.

## Source Excerpt
> In particular, the COVID-19 pandemic provided a unique opportunity to study air and water quality when economic activities were reduced as a result of local and global movement restrictions.
