# Maritime Singapore Decarbonisation Blueprint 2050


## Summary
A strategic blueprint developed by the Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) in partnership with industry stakeholders to decarbonise the maritime industry and reduce air pollution through low-carbon transitions.

## Key Data / Findings

| Targeted Sectors / Equipment | Mandated Actions | Transition Fuels | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Port equipment | Electrification | Electricity | Decarbonisation |
| Domestic harbour craft | Phase out diesel/bunker fuel | Low-carbon or zero-carbon energy sources | Decarbonisation |
| Singapore-flagged ships | Phase out diesel/bunker fuel | Low-carbon or zero-carbon energy sources | Decarbonisation |

> In addition, the Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) is developing the Maritime Singapore Decarbonisation Blueprint 2050 in partnership with industry stakeholders. Some initiatives include the electrification of port equipment, as well as transitioning domestic harbour craft and Singapore-flagged ships away from diesel or bunker fuel towards low-carbon or zero-carbon energy sources.
