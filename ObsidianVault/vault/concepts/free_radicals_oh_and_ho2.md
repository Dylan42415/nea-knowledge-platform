# Free radicals (OH. and HO2.)


## Summary
Atmospheric species that contribute to the formation of O3; their removal is suppressed when PM2.5 levels are low.

## Relationships
- **CONTRIBUTES_TO** → [[O3]]

## Source Excerpt
> suppressed the removal of active atmospheric free radicals such as hydroxyl radicals (OH.) and hydroperoxyl radicals (HO2.), contributing to enhanced formation of O3
