# National Environment Agency


## Summary
The National Environment Agency (NEA) is the statutory board responsible for improving and sustaining clean air and water in Singapore, managing emissions, operating PM2.5 networks, and enforcing industrial and vehicle regulations.

## Key Data / Findings

| Responsibility | Scope | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Air & Water Quality | National | Ambient air, inland and coastal water monitoring |
| Emissions Regulation | Industrial & Transport | VES, CVES, EEAI incentives, industrial surveillance |
