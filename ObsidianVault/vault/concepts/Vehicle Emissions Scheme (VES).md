# Vehicle Emissions Scheme (VES)


## Summary
An incentive and surcharge scheme for passenger vehicles enhanced in January and July 2021 to promote cleaner vehicle adoption.

## Key Data / Findings

| Implementation Date | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| January 2021 | Enhanced with increased rebates |
| July 2021 | Higher surcharges introduced |
