# PM2.5 (Fine Particulate Matter)


## Summary
PM2.5 refers to fine particulate matter capable of penetrating deep into lungs to cause cardiovascular and respiratory illness. NEA trialed low-cost PM2.5 sensors based on laser light scattering technology and conducted a 5-year characterization study with NERI to analyze its chemical constituents.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Trial/Model Value | Benchmark / Guideline |
|---|---|---|
| PM2.5 Low-Cost Sensor R² | 0.73 | US EPA Target ≥ 0.7 |
| PM2.5 Low-Cost Sensor RMSE | 3.14 µg/m³ | US EPA Target ≤ 7 µg/m³ |
| PM2.5 Dispersion Model R² Range | 0.70 to 0.97 | US EPA Target ≥ 0.7 |
| PM2.5 Dispersion Model RMSE Range | 1.80 to 3.70 µg/m³ | US EPA Target ≤ 7 µg/m³ |
| WHO Air Quality Guideline (AQG) (2005) | Within 25 µg/m³ (at trial expressway and intersection locations) | 25 µg/m³ |
