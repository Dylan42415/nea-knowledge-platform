# Benzene


## Summary
Benzene is a carcinogenic volatile organic compound (VOC) found in petroleum products and motor vehicle exhaust. Singapore benchmarks its ambient levels against international standards, recording its lowest level of 0.28 ppb in 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Annual Mean | 0.28 ppb | Lowest recorded since 2015 |
| 5-Year Ambient Range (2015-2019) | ~0.3 - 0.5 ppb | Below EU guidelines |
| Industrial Emission Limit | 5 mg/Nm³ | NEA Regulation |
| Petrol Content Limit | 1% by volume | Implemented in 2017 |
| WHO Ambient Exposure Limit | None established | - |
