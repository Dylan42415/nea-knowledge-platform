# Benzene


## Summary
Benzene is a carcinogenic volatile organic compound (VOC) occurring naturally in crude petroleum and emitted via industrial processes and vehicle exhaust. In 2020, Singapore recorded an average ambient benzene concentration of 0.28 ppb, the lowest since monitoring began in 2015.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Metric | Value | Benchmark / Standard | Implementation / Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 Annual Ambient Mean | 0.28 ppb | Lowest recorded since 2015 | Driven by lower petrochemical/transport activity |
| 5-Year Ambient Concentration Range | ~0.3 – 0.5 ppb | Below EU & international guidelines | Benchmark standard |
| Industrial Emission Limit | 5 mg/Nm³ | NEA Industrial Regulation | Mandatory limit |
| Petrol Content Limit | 1% by volume | Implemented in 2017 | Fuel standard regulation |
| WHO Ambient Exposure Limit | None set | N/A | Benchmarked against national limits |
