# Benzene

## Summary
Benzene is a volatile organic compound (VOC) monitored in Singapore due to its carcinogenicity. In 2020, the average ambient benzene concentration was 0.28 ppb, the lowest recorded since monitoring began in 2015.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Metric | 2020 Value / Limit | Context / Benchmark Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Annual Mean Concentration | 0.28 ppb | Lowest recorded since monitoring began in 2015 |
| 5-Year Historical Range (2015-2019) | 0.3 - 0.5 ppb | Below overseas guideline limits |
| Industrial Emission Limit | 5 mg/Nm³ | Imposed by NEA on industrial facilities |
| Petrol Content Limit | 1% by volume | Implemented by NEA in 2017 |
