# Benzene


## Summary
Benzene is a carcinogenic volatile organic compound (VOC) emitted through petrochemical processing, petroleum burning, motor vehicle exhausts, and tobacco smoke. In 2020, Singapore recorded an average ambient benzene concentration of 0.28 ppb, the lowest level since national monitoring began in 2015.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Parameter | Value | Guideline / Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Ambient Concentration | 0.28 ppb | Lowest recorded since 2015 |
| 5-Year Ambient Range | 0.3 – 0.5 ppb | Below EU Guideline limits |
| NEA Industrial Emission Limit | 5 mg/Nm³ | Industrial Regulation |
| Petrol Content Limit | 1% by volume | Implemented in 2017 |
