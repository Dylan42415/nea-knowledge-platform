# Sulphur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
Sulphur Dioxide is an air pollutant primarily emitted from industrial and shipping activities. Singapore's maximum 24-hour SO2 mean in 2020 was 30 µg/m³, which remains above the WHO AQG value of 20 µg/m³.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Maximum 24-hour Mean | 30 µg/m³ | WHO AQG (2005): 20 µg/m³ |
| Industrial Emission Standard | 1,700 mg/Nm³ (new plants 2015, existing plants by 2023) | NEA Regulation |
| Marine Fuel Sulphur Limit | 0.50%m/m (from 1 Jan 2020) | IMO Regulation |
| Diesel Vehicle Sulphur Limit | 0.001% (reduced from 0.005% in 2013) | NEA Regulation |
| Petrol Vehicle Sulphur Limit | 0.001% (reduced from 0.005% in 2017) | NEA Regulation |
