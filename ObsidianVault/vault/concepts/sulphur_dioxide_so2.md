# Sulphur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
A criteria air pollutant primarily emitted from industrial and shipping activities in Singapore.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| 2020 Max 24-hour mean | 30 µg/m³ |
| WHO AQG (2005) Value | 20 µg/m³ |

## Source Excerpt
> Singapore’s maximum 24-hour SO2 mean in 2020 was 30 µg/m3, the lowest over the past 10 years.
