# Sulphur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
Sulphur Dioxide (SO2) emissions in Singapore are primarily driven by industrial and shipping activities, recording a 10-year low maximum 24-hour mean of 30 µg/m³ in 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Max 24-hour Mean | 30 µg/m³ | Lowest over past 10 years |
| WHO AQG (2005) Value | 20 µg/m³ | WHO Guideline (Unmet) |
| New Industrial Plant Standard | 1,700 mg/Nm³ | Implemented 2015 (Existing by 2023) |
| Ship Fuel Sulphur Limit | 0.50%m/m | Implemented Jan 1, 2020 by IMO |
| Diesel Vehicle Fuel Sulphur Limit | 0.001% | Reduced from 0.005% in 2013 |
| Petrol Vehicle Fuel Sulphur Limit | 0.001% | Reduced from 0.005% in 2017 |

> Singapore’s maximum 24-hour SO2 mean in 2020 was 30 µg/m³, the lowest over the past 10 years. However, Singapore is still unable to meet the WHO AQG (2005) value of 20 µg/m³.
