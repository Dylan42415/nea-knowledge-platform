# Vehicle Emissions Scheme (VES)


## Summary
A regulatory scheme incentivizing cleaner passenger vehicles through rebates and surcharges based on emissions performance.

## Key Data / Findings

| Action | Effective Date |
| :--- | :--- |
| Increased Rebates | January 2021 |
| Higher Surcharges | 1 Jul 2021 |

## Source Excerpt
> The Vehicle Emissions Scheme (VES) was enhanced with increased rebates from January 2021 and higher surcharges from 1 Jul 2021
