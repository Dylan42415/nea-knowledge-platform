# Organic Carbon and Sulphates


## Summary
Organic carbon and sulphates were identified as the consistently highest chemical constituents of PM2.5 in Singapore. Organic carbon originates from anthropogenic and biogenic sources, while sulphates stem primarily from refinery and shipping emissions of sulphur dioxide.

## Key Data / Findings

| Component | Major Emission Sources | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Organic Carbon | Anthropogenic and biogenic sources | Highest constituent in PM2.5 characterization |
| Sulphates | Refineries and shipping (via Sulphur Dioxide) | Highest constituent in PM2.5 characterization |
