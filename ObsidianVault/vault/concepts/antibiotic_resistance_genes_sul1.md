# Antibiotic Resistance Genes (sul1)


## Summary
Concentrations of sul1 genes encoding resistance to sulphonamides relative to 16S rRNA bacterial genes were monitored in Singapore's marine waters as a proxy for antimicrobial resistance.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | International Comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Relative Concentration (sul1 / 16S rRNA) | 10⁻⁵ – 10⁻² | Comparable to South Korea (10⁻⁶–10⁻⁵), China (10⁻³), Japan (10⁻³), California & South Africa (10⁻⁷–10⁻²) |
