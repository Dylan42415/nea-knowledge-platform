# Sulfur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
Sulfur dioxide is an industrial air pollutant originating from major domestic sources such as oil refineries, power generation, and shipping. NEA applies stringent upstream controls and continuous monitoring to bring SO2 levels into compliance with WHO guidelines.
