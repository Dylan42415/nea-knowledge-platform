# Sulfur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
Sulfur Dioxide (SO2) is a primary air pollutant generated by industrial facilities, petroleum refineries, and shipping operations. Atmospheric SO2 concentrations fell by 58.1% during the 2020 Circuit Breaker, though full-year levels exceeded the WHO AQG (2005) target.
