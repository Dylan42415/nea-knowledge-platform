# Sulfur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
Sulfur dioxide (SO2) is an air pollutant emitted from industrial facilities, petroleum refineries, and shipping activities. SO2 concentrations fell by 58.1% during the 2020 Circuit Breaker period, though annual ambient levels exceeded the WHO AQG (2005) limits.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Parameter | Value | Reference / Status |
|---|---|---|
| Circuit Breaker Level Change | -58.1% | 7th April–1st June 2020 vs 2017–2019 |
| 2020 Annual Compliance | Exceeded | Did not meet WHO AQG (2005) limit |
