# Sulfur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
Sulfur Dioxide (SO2) is an industrial and atmospheric pollutant subject to domestic emissions controls and continuous air quality monitoring in Singapore. Its atmospheric column density is tracked using high-resolution geostationary satellite instruments.

## Key Data / Findings

| Property | Specification |
|---|---|
| Environmental Target | WHO final Air Quality Guidelines (2005) |
| Primary Satellite Instrument | GEMS (GEO-KOMPSAT 2B) |
