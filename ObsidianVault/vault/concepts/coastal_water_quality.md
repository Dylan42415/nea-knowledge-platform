# Coastal Water Quality


## Summary
Coastal water quality along the Straits of Singapore and Straits of Johor is monitored manually at 43 points and via 8 continuous real-time buoy stations.

## Key Data / Findings

| Monitoring Method | Location / Coverage | Key Parameters Tracked |
|---|---|---|
| Manual Water Sampling | 43 sampling points (Straits of Singapore & Straits of Johor) | Physical, chemical, biological, microbiological parameters |
| Continuous Buoy Stations | 8 buoy-based monitoring stations (Commissioned 2014) | Temperature, salinity, pH, dissolved oxygen, chlorophyll-a, turbidity (15 total parameters) |
