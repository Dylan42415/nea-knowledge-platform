# Peat Forest Smoke Indicator


## Summary
The peat forest smoke indicator is an analytical tool designed by NERI to determine the origin of haze in Singapore. It functions by validating unique fractions of organic carbon ratios inside PM2.5 carbonaceous profiles during regional fire events.

## Key Data / Findings

| Event Modeled | Application Date / Episode | Key Diagnostic Metric |
|---|---|---|
| Transboundary Haze | 2019 Haze Episode | Unique ratios of organic carbon |

## Source Excerpt
> During the 2019 haze episode, the peat forest smoke indicator developed by NERI was used and it established that PM2.5 mainly originated from peatland burning in the region as analysis conducted on it revealed higher fractions of the unique ratios of organic carbon in the carbonaceous profile of PM2.5.
