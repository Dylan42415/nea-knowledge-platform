# Carbonaceous Compounds


## Summary
Carbonaceous compounds comprising Organic Carbon (OC) and Elemental Carbon (EC) constitute the leading chemical class in smoke-laden PM2.5. The ratio between char-EC and soot-EC indicates whether smoldering peat fires or flaming ground fires predominate.

## Key Data / Findings

| Indicator Class | Predominant Condition | Associated Emissions |
|---|---|---|
| Char-EC > Soot-EC | Smoldering-prominent burning | Organic gases, particulates, underground peat fires |
| Soot-EC > Char-EC | Flaming-prominent burning | Inorganics, sulfates, heavy metals, ground forest fires |
| 2011–2015 Profile Trend | Increasing dominance of flaming-prominent smoke | Strongest extent recorded in 2015 |
| 2019 Profile Trend | Reversed to smoldering-dominant smoke | Attributed to El Niño & peatland re-wetting/restoration |
