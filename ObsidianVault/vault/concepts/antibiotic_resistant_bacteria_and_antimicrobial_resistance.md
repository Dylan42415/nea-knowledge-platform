# Antibiotic Resistant Bacteria and Antimicrobial Resistance


## Summary
Antimicrobial resistance driven by persistent exposure to antibiotics is recognized by the WHO as a major public health threat. In Singapore, coastal and aquaculture monitoring detected average concentrations of 10 CFU/100mL of ESBL-resistant E. coli.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Metric | Value | Comparison / Location |
|---|---|---|
| ESBL-resistant E. coli (Coastal/Aquaculture) | 10 CFU/100mL | Netherlands recreational waters (2010): 1 CFU/100mL |
| sul1 Gene Concentrations (relative to 16S rRNA) | $10^{-5}$ – $10^{-2}$ | South Korea ($10^{-6}$–$10^{-5}$), China ($10^{-3}$), Japan ($10^{-3}$) |
