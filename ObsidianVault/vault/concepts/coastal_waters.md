# Coastal Waters


## Summary
Coastal waters monitored across Straits of Johor West, Straits of Johor East, and Straits of Singapore met dissolved oxygen guidelines throughout 2020, while enterococcus levels fluctuated.

## Key Data / Findings

| Monitoring Region | Parameter | 2020 Performance / Observation |
|---|---|---|
| Straits of Johor (SOJ) West | Dissolved Oxygen | Met guidelines (≥ 4 mg/l) all months |
| Straits of Johor (SOJ) East | Dissolved Oxygen | Met guidelines (≥ 4 mg/l) all months |
| Straits of Singapore (SOS) | Dissolved Oxygen | Met guidelines (≥ 4 mg/l) all months |
| Coastal Waters (All zones) | Enterococcus | Failed guideline (200 counts/100ml) in May 2020 |
