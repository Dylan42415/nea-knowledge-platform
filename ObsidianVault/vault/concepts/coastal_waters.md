# Coastal Waters


## Summary
Coastal waters in Singapore are monitored across the Straits of Johor (East and West) and the Straits of Singapore. In 2020, Dissolved Oxygen (DO) met the 4 mg/l guideline in all months, while Enterococcus levels exceeded the 200 counts/100ml guideline in May 2020. Annual mean levels for both DO and Enterococcus remained relatively stable over the 10-year period from 2011 to 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Indicator | 2020 Performance | Guideline / Benchmark | 10-Year Trend (2011–2020) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dissolved Oxygen (DO) | Met guideline for all 12 months | ≥ 4 mg/l (AMWQC) | Relatively stable |
| Enterococcus | Exceeded guideline in May 2020 | ≤ 200 counts/100ml (WHO) | Relatively stable, 3-year fluctuations |
