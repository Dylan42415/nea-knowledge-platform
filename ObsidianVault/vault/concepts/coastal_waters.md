# Coastal Waters


## Summary
Singapore's coastal waters, monitored across the Straits of Johor (SOJ) East, SOJ West, and the Straits of Singapore (SOS), are evaluated monthly and annually to assess ecological health and public safety compliance.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Guideline | 2020 Performance | Long-Term Trend (2011-2020) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dissolved Oxygen (DO) | &ge; 4 mg/l | Met guidelines for all months of 2020 | Remained relatively stable |
| Enterococcus | &le; 200 counts per 100ml | Did not meet guideline in May 2020 (fluctuated along SOJ West, SOJ East, and SOS) | Remained relatively stable |

## Source Excerpt
> DO in coastal waters met the water quality guidelines of 4mg/l for all months of 2020. Water quality did not meet the enterococcus guideline of 200 counts per 100ml in May 2020. The monthly mean enterococcus levels fluctuated over the past 3 years along Straits of Johor (SOJ) West, SOJ East and Straits of Singapore (SOS).
