# Enterococcus


## Summary
Enterococcus is monitored as an indicator of faecal pollution in recreational beach waters to check compliance with WHO guidelines for primary contact water activities.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Specification | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Bacteria Size | 0.6 to 2.0 micrometres in diameter | >1,000 bacteria fit in head of a safety pin |
| Application | Indicator of faecal pollution | WHO recreational water quality guidelines |
| Monitoring Frequency | Weekly | NEA Beach Water Quality Information System (BSWI) |
