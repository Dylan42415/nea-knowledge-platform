# Enterococcus


## Summary
Enterococcus is a bacteriological indicator present in the faeces of humans and warm-blooded animals used to monitor faecal contamination and prevent gastrointestinal or respiratory infections in water users. Threshold guidelines set a maximum limit of 200 counts/100ml based on WHO recreational water quality guidelines.
