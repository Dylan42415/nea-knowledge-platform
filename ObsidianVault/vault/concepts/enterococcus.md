# Enterococcus


## Summary
Enterococcus is monitored as an indicator of faecal pollution in recreational beaches to check for compliance with WHO recreational water quality guidelines for primary contact activities.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Specification | Benchmark / Context |
|---|---|---|
| Indicator Type | Faecal pollution | WHO recreational water quality guidelines |
| Size | 0.6 to 2.0 micrometres | More than 1,000 fit into the head of a small safety pin |
