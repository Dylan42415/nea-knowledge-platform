# Enterococcus


## Summary
Enterococcus is a bacterium sized 0.6 to 2.0 micrometres used as an indicator of faecal pollution to monitor compliance with WHO recreational water quality guidelines at beaches.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Specification / Detail |
|---|---|
| Size | 0.6 to 2.0 micrometres in diameter |
| Indicator Purpose | Faecal pollution for primary contact water activities |
| Benchmark | WHO recreational water quality guidelines |
