# Enterococcus


## Summary
A bacteria type monitored in coastal waters with a health guideline of 200 counts per 100ml. Individual bacteria measure 0.6 to 2.0 micrometres in diameter.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Guideline | 200 counts per 100ml |
| Diameter | 0.6 to 2.0 micrometres |

## Source Excerpt
> Water quality did not meet the enterococcus guideline of 200 counts per 100ml in May 2020.
