# Enterococcus


## Summary
Enterococcus is a bacterium used as an indicator of faecal pollution to check beach water compliance with WHO recreational water quality guidelines for primary contact activities.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Cell Size | 0.6 to 2.0 micrometres | Diameter of individual bacteria |
| Indicator Use | Faecal pollution | Tested weekly at 7 popular recreational beaches |
