# Enterococcus


## Summary
Enterococcus is a bacteria measuring 0.6 to 2.0 micrometres in diameter used as a biological indicator of faecal pollution in beach water quality assessments. NEA tests for Enterococcus weekly across 7 popular recreational beaches to verify compliance with WHO guidelines.

## Key Data / Findings

| Property | Metric / Detail |
|---|---|
| Bacterial Cell Diameter | 0.6 to 2.0 micrometres |
| Relative Size Reference | >1,000 bacteria fit into the head of a small safety pin |
| Indicator Target | Faecal pollution |
| Sampling Frequency | Weekly across 7 beaches |
| Standard Benchmark | WHO recreational water quality guidelines for primary contact |
