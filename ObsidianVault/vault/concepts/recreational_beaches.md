# Recreational Beaches


## Summary
All seven monitored recreational beaches achieved a 'Good' banding for 2020 under NEA recreational water quality guidelines, remaining suitable for primary contact recreational activities.

## Key Data / Findings

| Monitored Beaches | 2020 Performance Banding | Governing Guideline |
|---|---|---|
| Changi beach, East Coast Park beach, Pasir Ris beach, Punggol beach, Seletar Island beach, Sentosa Island beach, Sembawang Park beach | "Good" | 95th percentile Enterococcus ≤ 200 counts / 100 ml (WHO / NEA) |
