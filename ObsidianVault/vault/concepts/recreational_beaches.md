# Recreational Beaches


## Summary
Singapore monitors seven popular recreational beaches (Changi beach, East Coast Park beach, Pasir Ris beach, Punggol beach, Seletar Island beach, Sentosa Island beach, and Sembawang Park beach) for enterococcus bacteria. In 2020, all seven beaches achieved a 'Good' banding under NEA's guidelines (95th percentile ≤200 counts/100ml) and were suitable for primary contact activities.
