# Ground-Level Ozone


## Summary
Ground-level ozone is a secondary air pollutant formed via precursors such as carbon monoxide, monitored via 8-hour means against WHO guidelines.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| WHO AQG (2005) | 100 µg/m³ | 8-hour Mean Benchmark |
