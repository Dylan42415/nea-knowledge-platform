# Ground-Level Ozone


## Summary
Ground-level ozone is a secondary pollutant formed via precursors such as carbon monoxide and nitrogen oxides, monitored via an 8-hour mean against the WHO guideline of 100 µg/m³.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| WHO AQG (2005) (8-hour Mean) | 100 µg/m³ | International health guideline |
