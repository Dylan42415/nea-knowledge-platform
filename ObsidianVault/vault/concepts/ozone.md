# Ozone


## Summary
A secondary pollutant formed in the troposphere via chemical reactions involving NOx and VOCs in the presence of sunlight. Singapore has struggled to meet the WHO AQG (2005) 8-hour mean for ozone.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| WHO AQG (2005) Max 8-hour mean | 100 µg/m³ |
