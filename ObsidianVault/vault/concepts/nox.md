# NOX


## Summary
Nitrogen oxides, acting as precursors in O3 formation. High concentrations typically characterize urban areas due to vehicle emissions.

## Source Excerpt
> Urban areas are generally characterised by a low VOC/NOX ratio due to high NOX concentrations driven by emissions from vehicles
 — soe_report.pdf, p. 22
