# Inland Water Quality


## Summary
Inland water quality is monitored by the NEA across 74 sampling locations in catchment and non-catchment areas using physical, chemical, biological, and microbiological parameters.

## Key Data / Findings

| Monitoring Parameter | Scope & Coverage | Sampling Frequency | Governing Standards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catchment & Non-Catchment Waters | 74 sampling locations | Fortnightly to quarterly basis | NEA / ASEAN Strategic Plan (2005) |
| Parameters Tested | Physical, chemical, biological, microbiological | Routine & continuous sensors | International standards |
