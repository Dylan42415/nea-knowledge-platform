# Antimicrobial Resistance


## Summary
Antimicrobial resistance in Singapore's water bodies was monitored between 2015 and 2019 using ESBL-resistant Escherichia coli and antibiotic resistance genes (such as sul1) as proxies. ESBL-producing E. coli averaged 10 CFU/100mL in local coastal and aquaculture waters.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Indicator | Location / Water Type | Measured Concentration / Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| ESBL-resistant E. coli | Singapore coastal & aquaculture sites (2015–2019) | 10 CFU/100mL (average) |
| ESBL-resistant E. coli Benchmark | Netherlands recreational waters (2010) | 1 CFU/100mL (average) |
| Relative sul1 gene concentration | Singapore marine waters | 10⁻⁵ – 10⁻² (relative to 16S rRNA) |
| Relative sul1 gene Benchmark | South Korea aquaculture | 10⁻⁶ – 10⁻⁵ |
| Relative sul1 gene Benchmark | China & Japan aquaculture | 10⁻³ |
| Relative sul1 gene Benchmark | California harbour & ballast waters | 10⁻⁷ – 10⁻² |
| Relative sul1 gene Benchmark | South Africa harbour waters | 10⁻⁴ – 10⁻² |
| Relative sul1 gene Benchmark | China harbour waters | 10⁻¹ – 10¹ |
