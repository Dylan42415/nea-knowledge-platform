# Carbon Monoxide (CO)


## Summary
Carbon Monoxide is formed from incomplete combustion of carbon-containing fuels. Singapore's maximum 8-hour mean in 2020 was 1.2 mg/m³, remaining well within the WHO guideline of 10 mg/m³.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Max 8-hour Mean | 1.2 mg/m³ | WHO AQG (2005): 10 mg/m³ |
| Industrial Standard (New Plants, 2015/2023) | 250 mg/Nm³ | NEA Standard ( tightened from 625 mg/Nm³ ) |
