# Carbon Monoxide (CO)


## Summary
Carbon Monoxide is formed from incomplete combustion and serves as an ozone precursor; Singapore's maximum 8-hour mean in 2020 was 1.2 mg/m³, well within the WHO guideline of 10 mg/m³.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Standard | Value | Target / Guideline |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Maximum 8-hour Mean | 1.2 mg/m³ | WHO AQG (2005): 10 mg/m³ |
| Industrial CO Standard (New Plants) | 250 mg/Nm³ | Tightened in 2015 from 625 mg/Nm³ |
| Industrial CO Standard (Existing Plants) | 250 mg/Nm³ | Target by 2023 |
| Older Motorcycle Contribution | 50% of vehicle CO emissions | Accounts for 15% of local vehicle population |
