# Char-EC


## Summary
Char-EC is a subtype of elemental carbon in PM2.5. A high proportion of char-EC relative to soot-EC indicates smoldering-prominent combustion, which is characteristic of underground peatland burning.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Indicator | Value / Observation | Source / Context |
|---|---|---|
| Dominance Shift (2019) | Smoldering-dominant smoke | Lan et al. 2021 (El Niño year, possible peatland restoration effect) |
| Emission characteristics | More organic gases and particulates | Page and Hooijer 2016, Stockwell et al. 2016 |
