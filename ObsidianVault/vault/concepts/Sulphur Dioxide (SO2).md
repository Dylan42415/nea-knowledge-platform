# Sulphur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
Sulphur Dioxide is an air pollutant primarily emitted by industrial and shipping activities. Singapore's maximum 24-hour mean in 2020 was 30 µg/m³, which is the lowest in 10 years but still above the WHO guideline of 20 µg/m³.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Max 24-hour Mean | 30 µg/m³ | WHO AQG (2005): 20 µg/m³ |
| New Industrial Plant Standard (2015/2023) | 1,700 mg/Nm³ | NEA Industrial Regulation |
| Ship Fuel Sulphur Limit (from Jan 1, 2020) | 0.50% m/m | IMO Regulation |
| Diesel Vehicle Fuel Sulphur Limit (from 2013) | 0.001% | National Standard |
| Petrol Vehicle Fuel Sulphur Limit (from 2017) | 0.001% | National Standard |
