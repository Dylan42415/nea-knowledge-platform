# Sulphur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
Sulphur Dioxide is a criteria air pollutant primarily emitted by industrial and shipping activities, recording a maximum 24-hour mean of 30 µg/m³ in 2020 (the lowest in 10 years, though still above the WHO guideline of 20 µg/m³).

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Standard | Value | Target / Guideline |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Maximum 24-hour Mean | 30 µg/m³ | WHO AQG (2005): 20 µg/m³ |
| Industrial SO2 Standard (New Plants) | 1,700 mg/Nm³ | Implemented in 2015 |
| Industrial SO2 Standard (Existing Plants) | 1,700 mg/Nm³ | Target by 2023 |
| Marine Fuel Sulphur Limit | 0.50%m/m | Implemented 1 Jan 2020 by IMO |
| Diesel Vehicle Fuel Sulphur Limit | 0.001% | Reduced from 0.005% in 2013 |
| Petrol Vehicle Fuel Sulphur Limit | 0.001% | Reduced from 0.005% in 2013, further to 0.001% in 2017 |
