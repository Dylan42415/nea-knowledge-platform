# Geostationary Environmental Monitoring Spectrometer (GEMS)


## Summary
GEMS is a satellite mission launched in 2019 by the Republic of Korea onboard GEO-KOMPSAT 2B. Operating in the UV-visible spectral range, it produces high temporal and spatial resolution estimates of AOD and column amounts of O3, NO2, and SO2 across Asia.
