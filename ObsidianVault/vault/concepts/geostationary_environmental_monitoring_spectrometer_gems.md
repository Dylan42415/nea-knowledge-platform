# Geostationary Environmental Monitoring Spectrometer (GEMS)


## Summary
A mission launched by the Republic of Korea in 2019 onboard the GEO-KOMPSAT 2B satellite to provide atmospheric chemistry measurements over Asia.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Measurement Capability |
| :--- | :--- |
| Data Type | AOD, Column amounts of O3, NO2, SO2, and other tropospheric gases |
