# Geostationary Environmental Monitoring Spectrometer (GEMS)


## Summary
GEMS is an atmospheric chemistry satellite spectrometer launched by the Republic of Korea onboard the GEO-KOMPSAT 2B satellite, designed to track regional aerosol optical depth and column levels of atmospheric trace gases over Asia.

## Key Data / Findings

| Specification | Value | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Launch Year | 2019 | Republic of Korea space mission |
| Carrier Platform | GEO-KOMPSAT 2B satellite | Geostationary orbit |
| Spectral Range | UV-visible range | High temporal and spatial resolution |
| Monitored Gases | O3, NO2, SO2 | Tropospheric gases relevant to regional air quality |
