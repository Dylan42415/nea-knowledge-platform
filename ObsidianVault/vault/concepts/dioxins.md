# Dioxins


## Summary
Dioxins comprise polychlorinated dibenzo-p-dioxins (PCDDs) and polychlorinated dibenzofurans (PCDFs), highly toxic persistent organic pollutants (POPs) emitted via waste incineration and fuel combustion. In 2020, Singapore recorded an ambient concentration of 0.8 fg-TEQ/m³, its lowest in a decade and well below Japan's safety standard of 600 fg-TEQ/m³.
