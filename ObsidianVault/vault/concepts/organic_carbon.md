# Organic Carbon


## Summary
Organic carbon is a major chemical component of PM2.5 in Singapore, originating from both anthropogenic and biogenic activities. Unique ratios in its carbonaceous profile are analyzed to identify transboundary smoke sources.

## Key Data / Findings

| Property / Application | Details |
|---|---|
| Concentration Share | Consistently one of the two highest constituents of PM2.5 |
| Source Types | Anthropogenic (human-induced) and Biogenic (natural) |
