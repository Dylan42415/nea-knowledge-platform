# Ground-level Ozone (O3)


## Summary
Ground-level Ozone (O3) is a secondary air pollutant formed in the troposphere via complex photochemical reactions involving NOX and VOCs in the presence of sunlight. In 2020, ozone levels increased globally and locally, rising +12.0% in Singapore during the COVID-19 Circuit Breaker period despite primary pollutant drops.
