# PM10


## Summary
PM10 is particulate matter with diameters of 10 micrometers or less. Singapore is actively tracking and working to meet international guidelines for PM10, utilizing dispersion modeling for transboundary source attribution.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Parameter | Standard / Output | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Quality Benchmark | WHO final Air Quality Guidelines (2005) | Singapore target for cleaner ambient air |
| Source Attribution Model | NAME Model | Identifies biomass burning PM10 from Southeast Asia |
| Forecast Availability | Surface concentrations of PM10 and PM2.5 | Hosted at WMO VFSP-WAS regional centre portal |
