# PM10


## Summary
Particulate matter with a diameter of 10μm or below. It is a major air pollutant monitored by the NEA.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric | Threshold |
| :--- | :--- |
| WHO AQG (Annual Mean) | 20 µg/m³ |

## Source Excerpt
> PM10 refers to particulate matter of size 10μm or below in diameter.
