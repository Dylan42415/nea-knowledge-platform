# Coastal Waters


## Summary
Singapore's coastal waters are monitored across Straits of Johor (SOJ) West, SOJ East, and Straits of Singapore (SOS). Parameters evaluated include Dissolved Oxygen (DO) and Enterococcus levels.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Guideline | 2020 Performance |
|---|---|---|
| Dissolved Oxygen (DO) | ≥ 4 mg/l | Met guidelines for all months of 2020 |
| Enterococcus | ≤ 200 counts/100 ml | Exceeded guideline in May 2020 |
