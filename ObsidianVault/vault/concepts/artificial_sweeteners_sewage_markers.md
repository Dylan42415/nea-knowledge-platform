# Artificial Sweeteners (Sewage Markers)


## Summary
Artificial sweeteners—specifically acesulfame, cyclamate, and saccharin—serve as conservative chemical markers in Singapore's toolbox for diagnosing diffuse water pollution. Because of their unique environmental persistence and low attenuation during wastewater treatment, they reliably differentiate sewer leakage from surface runoff or illicit discharges.
