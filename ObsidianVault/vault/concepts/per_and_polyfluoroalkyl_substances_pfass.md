# Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFASs)


## Summary
Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFASs) are emerging contaminants found in packaging, firefighting foam, fabrics, and Teflon materials that exhibit bioaccumulative and carcinogenic properties.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Metric | Value | Benchmark / Comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Urban Water Total PFASs (Singapore) | <14.2 - 80 ng/L | Comparable to Tokyo Bay and South Korean river estuaries; lower than Sweden and Beijing |
| PFOS Concentration | <24 ng/L | USEPA Health Advisory (70 ng/L combined with PFOA) |
| PFOA Concentration | <21 ng/L | USEPA Health Advisory (70 ng/L combined with PFOS) |
