# Artificial Sweeteners


## Summary
Artificial sweeteners such as acesulfame, cyclamate, and saccharin serve as chemical markers to identify diffuse sources of pollution in urban surface waters. They enable water managers to distinguish sewer leakage from surface runoff or illicit discharges.

## Key Data / Findings

|---|---|---|
| Acesulfame, Cyclamate, Saccharin | Marker for sewage leakage in surface waters | Detection frequency, detection ratio, attenuation rates in WWTP, labile-to-conservative ratio |
