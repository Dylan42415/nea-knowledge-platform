# Emerging Contaminants


## Summary
Emerging contaminants (ECs) represent synthetic or naturally derived water pollutants—including pharmaceuticals, PPCPs, PFAS, EDCs, and nanoparticles—that pose potential eco-toxicological and human health risks. They are present in urban surface waters at trace levels ranging from ng/L to µg/L.

## Key Data / Findings

| Class / Parameter | Concentration Range | Key Discharging Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Urban Watershed Concentration | ng/L to µg/L | WWTP effluent, sewer leakages, landfills, runoff |
| Selected Marker Classes | Persistent organics, PPCPs, PFAS, EDCs, nanoparticles | Urban water cycle, industrial sites, firefighting facilities |
