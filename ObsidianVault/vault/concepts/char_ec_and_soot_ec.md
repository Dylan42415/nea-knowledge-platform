# Char-EC and Soot-EC


## Summary
Elemental carbon (EC) in smoke-laden PM2.5 is categorized into char-EC and soot-EC to diagnose fire origins. A higher char-EC ratio indicates smoldering underground peat fires, whereas a higher soot-EC ratio indicates flaming ground-level forest fires emitting inorganics, heavy metals, and sulfates.

## Key Data / Findings
