# Char-EC and Soot-EC


## Summary
Char-like elemental carbon (Char-EC) and soot-like elemental carbon (Soot-EC) are chemical subtypes of elemental carbon in PM2.5 used to identify the origin and combustion conditions of biomass burning. High Char-EC indicates low-temperature smoldering underground peat fires, whereas high Soot-EC signifies high-temperature flaming surface fires.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Indicator | Smoldering-Prominent Burning | Flaming-Prominent Burning |
|---|---|---|
| Dominant EC Type | Char-EC > Soot-EC | Soot-EC > Char-EC |
| Associated Fire Type | Underground peatland burning | Ground-level forest fires |
| Major Emissions | Organic gases and particulates | Inorganics (sulfates, heavy metals) |
| Observed Trend (2011–2015) | Declining proportion | Increasing dominance (peak in 2015) |
| Observed Trend (2019) | Reversed to smoldering-dominant | Reduced relative proportion |
