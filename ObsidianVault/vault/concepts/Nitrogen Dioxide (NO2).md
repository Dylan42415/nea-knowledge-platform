# Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2)


## Summary
The annual NO2 mean of 20 µg/m3 in 2020 was the lowest it has been in the last 10 years.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Annual Mean | 20 µg/m³ | WHO AQG: 40 µg/m³ |
| Industrial Standard (New Plants, 2015/2023) | 400 mg/Nm³ | NEA Standard ( tightened from 700 mg/Nm³ ) |
