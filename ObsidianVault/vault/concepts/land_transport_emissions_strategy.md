# Land Transport Emissions Strategy


## Summary
Singapore's strategy to reduce vehicular air emissions under the SG Green Plan 2030 includes expanding active mobility infrastructure and phasing out internal combustion engine vehicles. Policy levers include restrictions on older motorcycles, incentives for electric vehicles, and progressive phase-outs of diesel models.

## Key Data / Findings

| Policy / Infrastructure Measure | Metric / Target Year |
|---|---|
| Rail Network Expansion | 230 km (2021) → 360 km (early 2030s) |
| Cycling Network Expansion | 460 km (2020) → 1,320 km (2030) |
| Motorcycle Standards (Pre-1 Jul 2003) | Tighter in-use rules (Apr 2023); Road ban after 30 June 2028 |
| Phase Out New Diesel Cars & Taxis | Effective 2025 |
| Mandatory Cleaner Energy New Registrations | Effective 2030 |
| Government Fleet Electrification Target | All new cars clean from 2023; 100% clean fleet by 2035 |
