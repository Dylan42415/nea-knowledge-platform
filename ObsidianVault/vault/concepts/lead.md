# Lead


## Summary
Lead is a toxic heavy metal pollutant previously emitted mainly through leaded petrol combustion. Since leaded petrol was phased out in 1998, ambient lead levels in Singapore have remained well below the WHO Air Quality Guideline standard, recording 0.01 µg/m³ in 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Parameter | Value | Benchmark / Standard | Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 Ambient Concentration | 0.01 µg/m³ | WHO AQG: 0.5 µg/m³ | Consistent over past decade |
| Post-1998 Ambient Concentration | < 0.1 µg/m³ | WHO AQG: 0.5 µg/m³ | Stabilized after leaded petrol phase-out |
| Leaded Petrol Phase-Out Year | 1998 | Industrial/Fuel Regulation | Primary source elimination |
