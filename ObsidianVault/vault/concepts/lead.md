# Lead


## Summary
Lead is a heavy metal air pollutant historically emitted primarily from leaded petrol combustion. Following the complete phase-out of leaded petrol in Singapore in 1998, ambient lead levels have stabilized below 0.1 µg/m³, recording 0.01 µg/m³ in 2020, which is far below the WHO AQG guideline value of 0.5 µg/m³.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Threshold | Value | Reference Standard |
|---|---|---|
