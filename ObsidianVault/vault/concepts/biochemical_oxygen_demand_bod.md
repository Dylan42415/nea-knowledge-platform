# Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)


## Summary
Biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) quantifies the oxygen utilized by aerobic microorganisms during organic matter decomposition, indicating organic pollution and potential wastewater contamination.

## Key Data / Findings

| Application / Water Type | Parameter | Guideline Value | Benchmark Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inland Waters (Class I) | Biochemical Oxygen Demand | 5 mg/l | 2005 ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action on Water Resources Management |
| Inland Waters (Class IV) | Biochemical Oxygen Demand | 10 mg/l | 2005 ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action on Water Resources Management |
| Non-catchment Waters | Biochemical Oxygen Demand | ≤ 10 mg/l | NEA Guidelines |
