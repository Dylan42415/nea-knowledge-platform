# Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)


## Summary
Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) measures oxygen consumed by organic material in waterbodies; levels generally met guidelines except during heavy rain episodes in December 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Body Type | Guideline Threshold | 2020 Exception / Note |
|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≤ 5 mg/l | Exceeded in Dec 2020 due to heavy rainfall washdown |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≤ 10 mg/l | Exceeded in Dec 2020 due to intense Nov–Dec rain episodes |
