# Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)


## Summary
Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) measures the amount of oxygen consumed by organic material in waterbodies, experiencing temporary elevations due to heavy rainfall and soil washdown.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Body Type | Guideline Threshold | 2020 Performance | Long-Term Trend (10-Year) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≤5 mg/l | Within guidelines except Dec 2020 | Remained within guidelines |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≤10 mg/l | Within guidelines except Dec 2020 | Remained within guidelines |
