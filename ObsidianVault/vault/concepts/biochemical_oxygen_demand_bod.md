# Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)


## Summary
Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) measures oxygen consumed by organic material in waterbodies, showing temporary guideline exceedances in December 2020 due to heavy rainfall and soil washdowns.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Body Type | Guideline Threshold | 2020 Performance Exceedance |
|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≤5 mg/l | Exceeded in Dec 2020 due to heavy rainfall |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≤10 mg/l | Exceeded in Dec 2020 due to heavy rainfall |
