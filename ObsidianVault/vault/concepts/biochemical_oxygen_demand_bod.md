# Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)


## Summary
Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) measures the amount of oxygen consumed by aerobic microorganisms in water bodies, serving as an indicator of organic pollution levels driven by heavy rainfall and soil washdowns.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Body Type | Guideline / Threshold | 2020 Performance | Long-Term Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≤5 mg/l | Within guidelines except Dec 2020 due to heavy rainfall | Remained within guidelines over the last 10 years |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≤10 mg/l | Within guidelines except Dec 2020 due to heavy rainfall | Remained within guidelines over the last 10 years |
