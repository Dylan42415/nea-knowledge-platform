# Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)


## Summary
Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) levels generally met guidelines over 10 years, except for an increase in December 2020 caused by heavy rainfall and organic washdown.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Body Type | Guideline Threshold | 2020 Performance | Anomalies / Observations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≤5 mg/l | Within monthly means except Dec 2020 | Exceeded in Dec 2020 due to heavy rainfall and land washdown |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≤10 mg/l | Within guidelines except Dec 2020 | Exceeded in Dec 2020 due to intense Nov-Dec rain episodes |
| Long-Term Trend | ≤5 mg/l (Catchment) / ≤10 mg/l (Non-catchment) | Remained within guidelines over the last 10 years | Stable historical performance |
