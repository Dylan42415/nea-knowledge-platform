# WHO Recreational Water Quality Guidelines


## Summary
The World Health Organization (WHO) Recreational Water Quality Guidelines specify microbiological safe limits to protect public health during primary contact water activities.

## Key Data / Findings

| Microorganism Indicator | Threshold Limit | Compliance Metric |
|---|---|---|
| Enterococcus | ≤ 200 counts/100 ml | 95th percentile benchmark for primary contact activity |

> Under the guidelines, primary contact activity is only allowed when the 95th percentile enterococcus bacteria counts of the recreational beach water do not exceed 200counts/100 ml.
