# Organic Carbon


## Summary
Organic carbon is a primary chemical component of PM2.5 in Singapore, originating from both biogenic and anthropogenic sources. Its relative carbonaceous ratios are analyzed to pinpoint the presence of regional biomass and peatland burning.

> Organic carbon can come from both anthropogenic and biogenic sources while sulphates are mainly generated from refineries and shipping... During the 2019 haze episode, the peat forest smoke indicator developed by NERI was used and it established that PM2.5 mainly originated from peatland burning in the region as analysis conducted on it revealed higher fractions of the unique ratios of organic carbon in the carbonaceous profile of PM2.5.
