# Ozone (O3)


## Summary
Ground-level ozone (O3) is a secondary pollutant formed in the troposphere via complex photochemical reactions involving NOx and VOCs in sunlight. During Singapore's 2020 COVID-19 Circuit Breaker, ozone levels increased by +12.0%, and Singapore continues to exceed the WHO AQG (2005) 8-hour mean target of 100 µg/m³.
