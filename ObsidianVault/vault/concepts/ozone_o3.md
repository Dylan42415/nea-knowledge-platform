# Ozone (O3)


## Summary
Ozone (O3) is a major criteria pollutant targeted for reduction in Singapore to meet global health standards. Column amounts of atmospheric O3 are monitored via geostationary satellite spectrometry across Asia.

## Key Data / Findings

| Indicator | Benchmark / Sensor |
|---|---|
| Target Standard | WHO final Air Quality Guidelines (2005) |
| Remote Sensing Platform | GEMS (GEO-KOMPSAT 2B) |
| Spectral Observation Range | UV-visible |
