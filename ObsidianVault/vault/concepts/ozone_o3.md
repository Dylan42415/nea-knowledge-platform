# Ozone (O3)


## Summary
Ground-level ozone (O3) is a secondary pollutant formed in the troposphere via complex photochemical reactions between nitrogen oxides (NOX) and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) in the presence of sunlight. In 2020, ambient ozone levels increased in Singapore and globally, rising by +12.0% during the two-month Circuit Breaker period due to altered atmospheric chemistry and reduced NOX levels.
