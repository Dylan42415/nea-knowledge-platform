# Stockholm Convention on Persistent Organic Pollutants


## Summary
The Stockholm Convention is an international multilateral environmental treaty aimed at eliminating or restricting persistent organic pollutants (POPs). Singapore became a signatory in 2001 and enforces strict industrial emission controls for POPs like dioxins under its framework.
