# Particulate Matter (PM2.5)


## Summary
PM2.5 comprises fine particles 2.5µm or less in diameter that penetrate deep respiratory tracts; included in PSI computations since 2014, 2020 annual means reached 10-year lows despite exceeding WHO guidelines.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Standard | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Annual Mean Level | Lowest over last 10 years | Exceeds WHO AQG (2005) |
| PM2.5 WHO AQG (Annual Mean) | 10 µg/m³ | WHO Air Quality Guideline |
| Inclusion in PSI | Included in 2014 | Alters baseline good range percentage |
