# Particulate Matter (PM2.5)


## Summary
Particulate Matter (PM2.5) consists of fine particles monitored continuously in Singapore's air quality program. Its diameter is 30 times smaller than that of a strand of human hair.

## Key Data / Findings

| Object / Particle | Diameter Value | Ratio to PM2.5 |
|---|---|---|
| Fine Particulate Matter (PM2.5) | ~1.67 to 2.33 microns (Derived) | 1x |
| Human Hair | 50 - 70 microns | 30x larger |
| Fine Beach Sand | 90 microns | ~38.5x to 54x larger |
| Monitoring Frequency | Continuous | N/A |
