# Emerging Contaminants (ECs)


## Summary
Emerging contaminants (ECs) are synthetic or naturally derived water contaminants including persistent organic pollutants, pharmaceuticals, personal care products (PPCPs), PFASs, and disinfection by-products that pose challenges to aquatic ecosystems and urban water cycles.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Metric | Value | Context |
|---|---|---|
| Detection Range in Urban Watersheds | ng/L to µg/L | Ubiquitous detection via advanced analytical techniques |
| Representative Compound Classes | POPs, hormones, EDCs, pharmaceuticals, PPCPs, PFASs, nanoparticles | Listed by US EPA |
