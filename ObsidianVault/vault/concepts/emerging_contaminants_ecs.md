# Emerging Contaminants (ECs)


## Summary
Emerging contaminants comprise synthetic or naturally derived water contaminants including persistent organic pollutants, pharmaceuticals, personal care products, PFAS, and antimicrobial resistance markers.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Detection Range in Urban Watersheds | ng/L to µg/L | Low concentrations causing potential long-term bioaccumulation and health effects |
