# Fine Particulate Matter (PM2.5)


## Summary
Fine Particulate Matter (PM2.5) consists of microscopic airborne particles emitted by vehicles, industry, and combustion sources. During Singapore's 2020 Circuit Breaker, outdoor PM2.5 levels dropped by 28.8% due to reduced road traffic, though overall 2020 annual levels still exceeded the WHO AQG (2005) limit.
