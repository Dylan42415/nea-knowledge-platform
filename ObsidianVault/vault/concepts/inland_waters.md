# Inland Waters


## Summary
Singapore's inland water quality is categorized and monitored based on Class I (stringent, potable-ready) and Class IV river standards, adopting regional frameworks to secure safe freshwater systems.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Class I Standard | Class IV Standard | Primary Reference Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dissolved oxygen (DO) | 5 mg/l | 3 mg/l | 2005 ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action |
| Total suspended solids (TSS) | 50 mg/l | 300 mg/l | 2005 ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action |
| Biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) | 5 mg/l | 10 mg/l | 2005 ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action |
