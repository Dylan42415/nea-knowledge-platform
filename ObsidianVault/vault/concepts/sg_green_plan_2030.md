# SG Green Plan 2030


## Summary
SG Green Plan 2030 is Singapore's national sustainability agenda driving land transport decarbonization, infrastructure expansion, and vehicle fleet greening. Key targets include expanding rail/cycling networks and phasing out internal combustion engine vehicles.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Target | Initial Baseline | Target / Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Rail Network Length | 230 km (2021) | 360 km (Early 2030s) |
| Cycling Network Length | 460 km (2020) | 1,320 km (2030) |
| New Government Vehicles | Cleaner energy models | From 2023 |
| Government Car Fleet | 100% cleaner energy | By 2035 |
| Diesel Cars & Taxis | No new registrations | From 2025 |
| New Car/Taxi Registrations | 100% cleaner energy models | From 2030 |
| ICE Vehicle Phase-Out | Complete phase-out | By 2040 |
