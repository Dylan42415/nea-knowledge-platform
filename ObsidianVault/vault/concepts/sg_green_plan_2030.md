# SG Green Plan 2030


## Summary
A national initiative to expand transport infrastructure, including rail and cycling networks, to reduce reliance on private vehicles.

## Key Data / Findings

| Infrastructure | 2020/2021 Baseline | 2030 Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Rail Network | ~230km (2021) | 360km (early 2030s) |
| Cycling Network | 460km (2020) | 1,320km (2030) |

## Relationships
- **LOCATED_IN** → [[Singapore]]

## Source Excerpt
> As part of the SG Green Plan 2030, the rail network will be expanded from around 230km in 2021 to 360km by the early 2030s, and the cycling network will be expanded from 460km in 2020 to 1,320km by 2030
 — soe_report.pdf, p. 60
