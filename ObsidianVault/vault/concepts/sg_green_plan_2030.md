# SG Green Plan 2030


## Summary
The SG Green Plan 2030 is a multi-sectoral national sustainability blueprint in Singapore, driving major initiatives to reduce land transport emissions by expanding clean public transport networks and transitioning to clean energy vehicle fleets.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Target Plan | Baseline State | Target Goal / Target Year |
|---|---|---|
| Rail Network Expansion | ~230 km (2021) | 360 km (Early 2030s) |
| Cycling Network Expansion | 460 km (2020) | 1,320 km (2030) |
| EEAI Rebate Scheme | Launched 1 Jan 2021 | Electric vehicle early tax incentives |
| Diesel Cars/Taxis Registrations | Active | Zero new registrations (From 2025 onwards) |
| New Cars/Taxis Model Requirements | Hybrid/Electric | 100% cleaner energy models (By 2030) |
| Government Vehicle Procurement | Normal | 100% cleaner energy (By 2035; all new cars from 2023) |
| Internal Combustion Engine Phase-Out | Active | Complete phase-out (By 2040) |
