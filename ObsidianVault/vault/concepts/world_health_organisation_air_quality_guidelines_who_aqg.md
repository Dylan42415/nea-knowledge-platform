# World Health Organisation Air Quality Guidelines (WHO AQG)


## Summary
The World Health Organisation Air Quality Guidelines (WHO AQG) are international health-based thresholds used by Singapore as its primary environmental benchmark.

## Key Data / Findings

| Benchmark / Standard Target | Target Horizon |
|---|---|
| WHO AQG (2005) | Long term |
