# Centre for Remote Imaging, Sensing and Processing


## Summary
CRISP (NUS) conducts research on environmental remote sensing technologies, satellite imagery analysis, and numerical modeling (such as WRF-Chem) for haze and smoke transport forecasting.

## Key Data / Findings

| Research Focus | Tools / Models |
|---|---|
| Satellite Remote Sensing | MODIS, Himawari 8/9, AERONET, MPLNET |
| Numerical Modeling | WRF with Chemistry (WRF-Chem V3.6) over 9x9 km² grid |
