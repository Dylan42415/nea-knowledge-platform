# PM2.5


## Summary
PM2.5 is fine particulate matter whose levels are heavily influenced by transport and human activities. During Singapore's 2020 COVID-19 Circuit Breaker, outdoor PM2.5 levels dropped by 28.8%, though overall 2020 levels exceeded WHO guidelines.
