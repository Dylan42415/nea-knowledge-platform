# PM2.5


## Summary
PM2.5 is a critical particulate pollutant monitored across Singapore via a ground network hosted by NEA and estimated using satellite Aerosol Optical Depth (AOD). During the severe haze episode in September 2019, 1-hour PM2.5 concentrations reached a peak of 159 µg/m³.

| Metric / Benchmark | Value / Target |
|---|---|
