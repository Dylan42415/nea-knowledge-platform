# PM2.5


## Summary
A criteria air pollutant monitored by the NEA, characterized as fine particulate matter with a diameter 30 times smaller than a strand of human hair.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Human Hair Diameter | 50-70 microns |
| PM2.5 vs Hair | 30 times smaller |

## Source Excerpt
> The diameter of one strand of hair is 30 times larger than the diameter of a fine particulate matter (pm2.5)
