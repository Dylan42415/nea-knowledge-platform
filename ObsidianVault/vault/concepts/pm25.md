# PM2.5


## Summary
PM2.5 is a key particulate matter pollutant monitored across Singapore via ground instrument networks and satellite remote sensing to evaluate air quality and severe haze episodes, such as the September 2019 episode which peaked at 159 µg/m³.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark / Context |
|---|---|---|
| Peak 1-hour Concentration | 159 µg/m³ | 14 September 2019, 3pm local time |
| WHO Final Air Quality Guidelines (2005) | Target threshold | Singapore is working towards meeting final guidelines |
