# PM2.5


## Summary
PM2.5 refers to fine particulate matter monitored in Singapore. The National Environment Agency (NEA) operates a ground network of PM2.5 instruments for direct/indirect air quality measurement and rapid policy making.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Date / Target | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peak 1-Hour Concentration | 159 µg/m3 | 14th September 2019, 3pm | Recorded during Singapore's 2019 haze episode |
| Simulation Grid Size (WRF-Chem) | 9 x 9 km2 | September 2019 | Custom regional smoke transport model grid |
| Regional Forecast Models | PM2.5 and PM10 | Near real-time | Distributed via WMO VFSP-WAS SEA regional centre |
| Compliance Guideline | WHO final Air Quality Guidelines (2005) | Progressive Target | Singapore is working towards meeting this standard |
