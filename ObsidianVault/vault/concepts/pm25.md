# PM2.5


## Summary
PM2.5 is a key air quality indicator and pollutant monitored via ground networks, satellites, and numerical models in Singapore. During the September 2019 haze episode, the NEA 1-hour PM2.5 concentration peaked at a maximum of 159 µg/m³ on September 14 at 3pm.
