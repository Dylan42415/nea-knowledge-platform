# Dissolved Oxygen (DO)


## Summary
Dissolved Oxygen (DO) is monitored in catchment and coastal waters; catchment DO showed a temporary decline from 2015 to 2017 due to El Niño weather conditions.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Body Type | Guideline Threshold | 2020 Performance | Long-Term Trend / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≥5 mg/l | Within guidelines | Declined 2015–2017 (El Niño), improved past 3 years |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≥3 mg/l | Within guidelines for all samples | Remained within guidelines (10 years) |
| Coastal Waters (Buoys) | N/A (Measured by sensor) | Tracked via 8 buoy-based stations | Part of 15 key coastal water quality parameters |
