# Dissolved Oxygen (DO)


## Summary
Dissolved Oxygen (DO) is a crucial chemical water quality parameter that experienced a historical decline from 2015 to 2017 due to El Niño weather before recovering in subsequent years.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Body Type | Guideline Threshold | 2020 Performance |
|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≥5 mg/l | Within monthly means throughout 2020 |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≥3 mg/l | Within guidelines for all samples |
