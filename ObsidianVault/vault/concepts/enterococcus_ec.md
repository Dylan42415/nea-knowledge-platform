# Enterococcus (EC)


## Summary
Bacteriological parameter used to assess recreational beach water quality and faecal contamination levels.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric | Threshold |
| :--- | :--- |
| 95th percentile enterococcus | Less than or equal to 200 counts/100 ml |

## Source Excerpt
> Under the guidelines, primary contact activity is only allowed when the 95th percentile enterococcus bacteria counts of the recreational beach water do not exceed 200counts/100 ml.
 — soe_report.pdf, p. 34, 35
