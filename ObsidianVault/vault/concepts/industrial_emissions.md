# Industrial Emissions


## Summary
Industrial emissions from major emitters like oil refineries, waste incineration plants, and toxic industrial waste facilities are managed by NEA through upstream siting controls, pollution measures, and regulatory surveillance.

## Key Data / Findings

| Management Strategy | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Upstream Controls | Siting requirements and pollution control measures at the planning stage |
| Surveillance | Inspection of industrial premises for regulatory compliance |
| Environmental Monitoring | Continuous monitoring of ambient air, inland waters, and coastal waters |
