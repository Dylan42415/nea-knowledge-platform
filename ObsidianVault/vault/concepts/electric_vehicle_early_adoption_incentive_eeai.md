# Electric Vehicle Early Adoption Incentive (EEAI)


## Summary
EEAI is a financial incentive policy introduced by the Singapore government to accelerate the transition to electric passenger vehicles. It provides tax rebates on the purchase of new electric cars or taxis.

## Key Data / Findings

| Policy Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Implementation Date | 1 January 2021 |
| Target Fleet | Passenger cars and taxis |
| Primary Mechanism | Tax rebate on purchase of new electric vehicle |
