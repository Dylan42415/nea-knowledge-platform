# Electric Vehicle Early Adoption Incentive (EEAI)


## Summary
A tax rebate program introduced to promote the purchase of new electric cars and taxis.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| Effective Date | 1 January 2021 |
