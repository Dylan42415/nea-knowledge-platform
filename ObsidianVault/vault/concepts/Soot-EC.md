# Soot-EC


## Summary
Soot-EC is a subtype of elemental carbon in PM2.5 indicating flaming-prominent combustion, such as on-ground forest fires, when it occurs in higher relative concentrations than char-EC.

## Key Data / Findings

| Metric / Indicator | Value / Observation | Source / Context |
|---|---|---|
| Peak Dominance Year | 2015 (strongest extent) | Lan et al. 2021 (flaming-prominent smoke trend since 2011) |
| Anthropogenic Driver | Expanded use of peatland >60% | Pristine peat-swamp forest loss by 2015 (Miettinen et al. 2016) |
| Emission characteristics | More inorganic emissions (sulfates, heavy metals) | Kim et al. 2018 |

> When the relative concentration flips with more abundant soot-EC, flaming (a possible indication of more ground-level burning such as on-ground forest fires) predominates with more emissions of inorganics such as sulfates and heavy metals (Kim et al. 2018).
