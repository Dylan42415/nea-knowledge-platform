# Particulate Matter 10 (PM10)


## Summary
PM10 refers to particulate matter of size 10µm or below in diameter that can be inhaled and deposited throughout the airways and lungs, with 2020 annual levels exceeding the WHO AQG but marking a 10-year low.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Performance | Lowest over the last 10 years | Exceeded WHO AQG (2005) |
| WHO AQG (Annual Mean) | 20 µg/m³ | WHO (2005) Guideline |
