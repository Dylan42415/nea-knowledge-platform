# COVID-19 Circuit Breaker


## Summary
The Circuit Breaker (CB) was a non-pharmaceutical intervention period in Singapore from 7th April to 1st June 2020 designed to control COVID-19 transmission. The sharp decline in human mobility and industrial traffic led to substantial reductions in primary air pollutants (PM2.5, NO2, SO2, CO) but an unexpected 12.0% increase in ground-level ozone.

## Key Data / Findings

| Air Quality Metric | Circuit Breaker Percentage Change (vs 2017–2019 Mean) |
|---|---|
| Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) | -21.4% |
| Fine Particulate Matter (PM2.5) | -28.8% |
| Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2) | -38.1% |
| Carbon Monoxide (CO) | -5.6% |
| Sulfur Dioxide (SO2) | -58.1% |
| Ground-level Ozone (O3) | +12.0% |
