# Particulate Matter 2.5 (PM2.5)


## Summary
PM2.5 comprises fine particles 2.5µm or less in diameter that penetrate deeper regions of the respiratory tract, included in Singapore's PSI computation since 2014.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 Performance | Lowest recorded over the last 10 years | Exceeded WHO AQG (2005) |
| WHO AQG (Annual Mean) | 10 µg/m³ | WHO (2005) Guideline |
| Inclusion in PSI | 2014 | NEA Implementation |
