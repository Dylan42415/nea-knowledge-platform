# Non-catchment Waters


## Summary
Non-catchment waters are monitored for Total Suspended Solids (TSS), Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD), and Dissolved Oxygen (DO). From 2011 to 2020, annual mean levels for TSS, BOD, and DO continuously met their respective water quality guidelines.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Guideline Benchmark | 2011–2020 Performance Trend |
|---|---|---|
| Total Suspended Solids (TSS) | ≤ 300 mg/l | Compliant (Well below limit) |
| Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) | ≤ 10 mg/l | Compliant |
| Dissolved Oxygen (DO) | ≥ 3 mg/l | Compliant |
