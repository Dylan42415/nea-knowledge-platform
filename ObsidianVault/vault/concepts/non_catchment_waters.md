# Non-catchment waters


## Summary
Water bodies assessed against specific water quality guidelines for BOD, DO, and TSS, aligned with ASEAN 2005 standards.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Guideline |
|---|---|
| DO | ≥3 mg/l |
| BOD | ≤10 mg/l |
| TSS | ≤300 mg/l |
