# In-Stack Emission Monitoring Systems


## Summary
In-stack emission monitoring systems are continuous monitoring installations mandated for incineration plants in Singapore. They allow NEA to track industrial emissions directly at the source and enforce industrial air emission limits.

## Key Data / Findings

| Requirement | Facility Applicability | Governing Body |
|---|---|---|
| Mandatory Continuous Monitoring | Incineration Plants | National Environment Agency (NEA) |
