# In-stack Emission Monitoring Systems


## Summary
In-stack emission monitoring systems are required technology solutions installed at incineration plants in Singapore to monitor emissions continuously as NEA reviews industrial air emission limits.

## Key Data / Findings

| Requirement | Facility Type |
|---|---|
| Mandatory In-stack Emission Monitoring | Incineration plants |
| Governing Body | National Environment Agency (NEA) |
