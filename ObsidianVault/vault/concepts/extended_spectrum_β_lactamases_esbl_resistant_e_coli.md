# Extended-Spectrum β-Lactamases (ESBL) Resistant E. coli


## Summary
ESBL-resistant Escherichia coli is an antibiotic-resistant bacterium monitored in Singapore's coastal and aquaculture sites between 2015 and 2019. Studies detected average concentrations of 10 CFU/100mL, which were higher than levels found in Dutch coastal recreational waters in 2010.
