# World Health Organisation Recreational Water Quality Guidelines


## Summary
The WHO Recreational Water Quality Guidelines evaluate health risks in recreational waters. Under these guidelines, primary contact activities are permitted when 95th percentile Enterococcus counts do not exceed 200 counts/100ml.

## Key Data / Findings

| Target Parameter | Threshold Limit | Statistical Metric | Activity Qualification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterococcus | 200 counts/100ml | 95th Percentile | Primary contact recreational activities |
