# Beach Short-term Water Quality Information (BSWI)


## Summary
A weekly water quality assessment program developed by the National Environment Agency (NEA) targeting popular recreational beaches in Singapore to provide current environmental safety data and advisories to the public.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Start Date | End of 2020 |
| Frequency | Weekly |
| Coverage | Singapore's seven popular recreational beaches |
| Deliverables | Assessment, advisories, and recommended precautions |

> Since the end of 2020, NEA has been providing Beach Short-term Water Quality Information (BSWI), which is a weekly assessment of water quality at Singapore’s seven popular recreational beaches. The assessment is accompanied with advisories and recommended precautions.
