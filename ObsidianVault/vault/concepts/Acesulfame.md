# Acesulfame


## Summary
Acesulfame is an artificial sweetener evaluated within Singapore's emerging contaminants toolbox. Due to its unique environmental properties, it is utilized as an ideal chemical tracer for identifying diffuse sources of sewage leakage in surface waters.

## Key Data / Findings

| Selection Criterion | Value / Parameter | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Marker Classification | Sewage Marker | Tracing sewer leakage, illicit discharges, or runoff |
| Selected Sweetener Cohort | Acesulfame, Cyclamate, Saccharin | Part of targeted chemical marker toolbox |
