# Short-term Beach Water Quality Information System (BSWI)


## Summary
Introduced in October 2020 by the NEA, the BSWI provides weekly water quality assessments and water quality banding advisories for 7 popular recreational beaches.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Details |
|---|---|
| Launch Date | October 2020 |
| Monitored Locations | 7 beaches (Sentosa Island, Seletar Island, Sembawang Park, Changi, East Coast Park, Pasir Ris, Punggol) |
| Portal | https://www.nea.gov.sg/beach-water-quality |
