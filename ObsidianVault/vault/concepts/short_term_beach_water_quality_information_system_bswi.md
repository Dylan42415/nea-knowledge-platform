# Short-term Beach Water Quality Information System (BSWI)


## Summary
Introduced by NEA in October 2020, the BSWI provides weekly water quality assessments and advisories across 7 major recreational beaches to help beachgoers make informed decisions.

## Key Data / Findings

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Launch Date | October 2020 |
| Covered Locations | 7 beaches (Sentosa Island, Seletar Island, Sembawang Park, Changi, East Coast Park, Pasir Ris, Punggol) |
| Output | Weekly assessments with water quality bandings and advisories |
