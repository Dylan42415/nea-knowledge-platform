# Short-term Beach Water Quality Information System (BSWI)


## Summary
Introduced in October 2020 by the NEA, the BSWI provides weekly assessments and water quality bandings for 7 popular recreational beaches.

## Key Data / Findings

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Launch Date | October 2020 |
| Covered Beaches | Sentosa Island, Seletar Island, Sembawang Park, Changi, East Coast Park, Pasir Ris, Punggol (7 beaches) |
| Function | Weekly assessments, water quality bandings, and public advisories for primary contact activities |
| Access URL | https://www.nea.gov.sg/beach-water-quality |
