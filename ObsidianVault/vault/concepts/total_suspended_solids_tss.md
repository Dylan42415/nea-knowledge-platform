# Total Suspended Solids (TSS)


## Summary
An indicator of water clarity that affects photosynthesis rates and the survival of benthic organisms by blocking sunlight.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Class I | Class IV |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Total suspended solids (mg/l) | 50 | 300 |

## Source Excerpt
> Total suspended solids (TSS) is an indicator of water clarity, which dictates photosynthesis rates and the survival of aquatic plants or benthic... organisms in the water.
