# Total Suspended Solids (TSS)


## Summary
Total Suspended Solids (TSS) is a physical water quality parameter monitored in catchment and non-catchment waters by the NEA, maintaining levels within guidelines over the 10-year period up to 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Body Type | Guideline Threshold | 2020 Performance |
|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≤ 50 mg/l | Within guidelines (monthly means within limits) |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≤ 300 mg/l | Within guidelines for all samples (2018–2020) |
