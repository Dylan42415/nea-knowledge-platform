# Total Suspended Solids (TSS)


## Summary
Total Suspended Solids (TSS) is a key physical water quality parameter monitored in catchment and non-catchment waters by the NEA, remaining within guideline limits over the long term.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Body Type | Guideline Threshold | 2020 Performance |
|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≤50 mg/l | Within guidelines for all monthly means |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≤300 mg/l | Within guidelines for all samples |
