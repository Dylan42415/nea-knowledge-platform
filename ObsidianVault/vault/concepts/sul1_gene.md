# sul1 Gene


## Summary
The sul1 gene encodes resistance to sulphonamides and is used as a genetic proxy for antimicrobial resistance in aquatic environments. Concentrations relative to 16S rRNA bacterial genes in Singapore marine waters range between 10⁻⁵ and 10⁻².
