# sul1 Gene


## Summary
The sul1 gene encodes resistance to sulphonamides and is utilized as a gene proxy to measure antimicrobial resistance levels in marine environments relative to 16S rRNA bacterial genes. Concentrations measured across Singapore marine water types range between 10⁻⁵ and 10⁻².
