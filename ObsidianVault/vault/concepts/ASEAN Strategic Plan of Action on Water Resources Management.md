# ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action on Water Resources Management


## Summary
Adopted in 2005, the ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action on Water Resources Management sets regional baseline quality criteria for Class I and Class IV inland river systems.

## Key Data / Findings

| Water Parameter | Class I Standard | Class IV Standard |
|---|---|---|
| Dissolved oxygen (mg/l) | 5 | 3 |
| Total suspended solids (mg/l) | 50 | 300 |
| Biochemical oxygen demand (mg/l) | 5 | 10 |
