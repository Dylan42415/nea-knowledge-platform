# ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action on Water Resources Management


## Summary
The 2005 ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action on Water Resources Management establishes regional baseline guidelines for surface water quality parameters. NEA's water quality standards for DO, BOD, and TSS in catchment and non-catchment waters are directly aligned with this framework.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Catchment Waters Guideline | Non-Catchment Waters Guideline |
|---|---|---|
| Dissolved Oxygen (DO) | ≥5 mg/l | ≥3 mg/l |
| Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) | ≤5 mg/l | ≤10 mg/l |
| Total Suspended Solids (TSS) | ≤50 mg/l | ≤300 mg/l |
