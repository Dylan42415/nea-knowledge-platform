# PFOA and PFOS


## Summary
Perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA) and perfluorooctane sulfonate (PFOS) are legacy long-chain hydrophobic PFAS compounds known for toxic and bioaccumulative properties. Concentrations detected in Singapore urban water bodies are strictly below the USEPA drinking water health advisory limits.

## Key Data / Findings

| Compound | Measured Ambient Concentration | Health Advisory / Standard |
|---|---|---|
| PFOS (Singapore Urban Water Body) | <24 ng/L | Combined USEPA Health Advisory: 70 ng/L |
| PFOA (Singapore Urban Water Body) | <21 ng/L | Combined USEPA Health Advisory: 70 ng/L |
| Combined PFOA + PFOS Target | <45 ng/L (max combined) | USEPA Drinking Water Advisory (70 ng/L) |
