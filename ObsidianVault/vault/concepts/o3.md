# O3


## Summary
Ozone is a complex atmospheric pollutant whose formation is enhanced by free radical pathways and reduced PM2.5 levels.

## Relationships
- **FORMED_VIA** → [[free radical pathways]]

## Source Excerpt
> the lower PM2.5 levels in Singapore presumably suppressed the removal of active atmospheric free radicals such as hydroxyl radicals (OH.) and hydroperoxyl radicals (HO2.), contributing to enhanced formation of O3 via the free radical pathways
