# PM2.5


## Summary
PM2.5 is fine particulate matter monitored continuously across Singapore, with individual strands of human hair measuring 30 times larger in diameter.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Value | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Pollutant Type | Criteria Air Pollutant / Fine Particulate Matter | WHO Air Quality Guidelines |
| Physical Dimension | Diameter is 30x smaller than a strand of human hair (50-70 microns) | Fine Beach Sand is 90 microns |
