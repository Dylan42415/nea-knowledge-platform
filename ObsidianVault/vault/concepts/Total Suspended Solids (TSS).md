# Total Suspended Solids (TSS)


## Summary
Total Suspended Solids (TSS) is an indicator parameter for water clarity and solid particulate content. In 2020, TSS monthly mean levels in both catchment and non-catchment waters fully complied with NEA guidelines and have remained compliant over the 10-year period from 2011 to 2020.

## Key Data / Findings

| Environment | Guideline Threshold | 2020 Performance | 10-Year Long-Term Performance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catchment Waters | ≤50 mg/l | Compliant for all monthly means | Maintained within guideline over last 10 years |
| Non-Catchment Waters | ≤300 mg/l | Compliant for all samples | Maintained within guideline over last 10 years |
