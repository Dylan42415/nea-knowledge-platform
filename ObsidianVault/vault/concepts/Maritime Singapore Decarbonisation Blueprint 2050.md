# Maritime Singapore Decarbonisation Blueprint 2050


## Summary
Developed by the Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) with industry stakeholders, this blueprint outlines initiatives to decarbonise Singapore's maritime sector, including port equipment electrification and transitioning harbour craft and ships to low- or zero-carbon energy sources.

## Key Data / Findings

| Initiative / Scope | Action / Strategy |
|---|---|
| Port Equipment | Electrification |
| Domestic Harbour Craft & Singapore-flagged Ships | Transition away from diesel or bunker fuel to low-carbon or zero-carbon energy sources |
| Lead Agency | Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) |
