# Saccharin


## Summary
Saccharin is an artificial sweetener serving as a key diagnostic chemical marker in Singapore's water management toolbox to identify sewage contamination and illicit discharges in surface waters.

## Key Data / Findings

| Diagnostic Role | Selection Criteria | Target Matrix |
|---|---|---|
| Anthropogenic Tracer | Biodegradation/sorption attenuation rates in wastewater treatment | Urban surface waters |
