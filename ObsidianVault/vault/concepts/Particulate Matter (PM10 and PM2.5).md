# Particulate Matter (PM10 and PM2.5)


## Summary
Particulate matter consists of solid and liquid particles in the air categorized by size, with PM10 being 10µm or below and PM2.5 being 2.5µm or less in diameter. In 2020, annual mean levels exceeded WHO guidelines but reached 10-year lows due to reduced industrial and vehicular activities.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter / Guideline | Value |
|---|---|
| PM10 WHO AQG (Annual Mean) | 20 µg/m³ |
| PM2.5 WHO AQG (Annual Mean) | 10 µg/m³ |
| 2020 Performance | Exceeded WHO AQG, lowest recorded over last 10 years |
