# Fine Particulate Matter (PM2.5)


## Summary
Fine particulate matter (PM2.5) consists of tiny, highly complex particles that can penetrate deep into the human respiratory system. From 2015 to 2020, NEA and NERI collaborated to study its properties, establishing organic carbon and sulphates as its highest chemical constituents and evaluating low-cost sensor technologies for vertical and localized dispersion monitoring.
