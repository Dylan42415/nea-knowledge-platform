# Fine Particulate Matter (PM2.5)


## Summary
Fine Particulate Matter (PM2.5) is an airborne pollutant generated from transport, power plants, and industrial activities. During Singapore's 2020 Circuit Breaker period, atmospheric PM2.5 levels dropped by 28.8%, though overall annual levels still exceeded WHO Air Quality Guidelines.
