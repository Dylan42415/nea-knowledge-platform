# Himawari8/9


## Summary
Himawari8/9 is a series of high-frequency geostationary imaging satellites operated by the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA). It captures full Asian hemisphere imagery every 10 minutes to track smoke dispersion and forest fire hotspots.
