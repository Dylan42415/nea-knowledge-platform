# Himawari8/9


## Summary
Himawari8/9 is a geostationary satellite series operated by the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA) that images the Asian hemisphere every 10 minutes. It provides high-resolution imaging at 2 km spatial resolution to monitor clouds, aerosols, and forest fire locations.
