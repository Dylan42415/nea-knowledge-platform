# Sulfur Dioxide (SO2)


## Summary
Sulfur Dioxide (SO2) is a primary pollutant emitted primarily by petroleum refineries, industrial operations, and shipping activities. SO2 concentrations fell by 58.1% during the 2020 Circuit Breaker period, though annual ambient levels exceeded the WHO AQG (2005) target.
