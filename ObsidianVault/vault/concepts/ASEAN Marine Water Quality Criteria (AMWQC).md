# ASEAN Marine Water Quality Criteria (AMWQC)


## Summary
The ASEAN Marine Water Quality Criteria (AMWQC) provides reference standards for coastal water quality to protect aquatic life, including a Dissolved Oxygen benchmark of 4 mg/l.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Guideline Value | Target Protection Domain |
|---|---|---|
| Dissolved Oxygen (DO) | 4 mg/l | Aquatic Life Protection |
