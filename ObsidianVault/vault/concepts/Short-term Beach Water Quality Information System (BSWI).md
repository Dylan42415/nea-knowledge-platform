# Short-term Beach Water Quality Information System (BSWI)


## Summary
The Short-term Beach Water Quality Information System (BSWI) was launched by NEA in October 2020 to provide weekly water quality assessments and advisory bandings for 7 recreational beaches. It assists beachgoers in making informed decisions regarding primary contact water activities.

## Key Data / Findings

| System Metric | Detail |
|---|---|
| Launch Date | October 2020 |
| Target Locations | 7 beaches (Sentosa Island, Seletar Island, Sembawang Park, Changi, East Coast Park, Pasir Ris, Punggol) |
| Information Frequency | Weekly assessment and advisories |
| System Access URL | https://www.nea.gov.sg/beach-water-quality |
