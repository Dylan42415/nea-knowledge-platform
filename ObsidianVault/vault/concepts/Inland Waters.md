# Inland Waters


## Summary
Inland water quality in Singapore is evaluated for catchment and non-catchment waters based on guideline values for Class I and Class IV rivers established by the 2005 ASEAN Strategic Plan of Action on Water Resources Management.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Class I Guideline | Class IV Guideline |
|---|---|---|
| Dissolved Oxygen (DO) | 5 mg/l | 3 mg/l |
| Total Suspended Solids (TSS) | 50 mg/l | 300 mg/l |
| Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD) | 5 mg/l | 10 mg/l |
