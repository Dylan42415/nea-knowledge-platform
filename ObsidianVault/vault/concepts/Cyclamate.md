# Cyclamate


## Summary
Cyclamate is an artificial sweetener and emerging contaminant identified as a suitable chemical marker to track sewage leakage in urban watersheds based on detection frequency and attenuation rates.

## Key Data / Findings

| Marker Purpose | Evaluation Metric | Focus Area |
|---|---|---|
| Sewage Leakage Detection | Concentration ratio of labile to conservative compounds | Singapore Urban Water Cycle |
