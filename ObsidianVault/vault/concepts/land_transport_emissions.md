# Land Transport Emissions


## Summary
Singapore's land transport emission reduction strategy focuses on expanding public transport and cycling infrastructure, greening vehicle fleets via financial incentives and regulatory schemes, and phasing out internal combustion engine vehicles by 2040.

## Key Data / Findings

| Initiative / Target | Detail / Timeline |
|---|---|
| Rail Network Expansion | Expanded from ~230 km in 2021 to 360 km by early 2030s (SG Green Plan 2030) |
| Cycling Network Expansion | Expanded from 460 km in 2020 to 1,320 km by 2030 |
| Electric Vehicle Early Adoption Incentive (EEAI) | Tax rebate on new electric cars/taxis from 1 January 2021 |
| Vehicle Emissions Scheme (VES) | Enhanced rebates (Jan 2021) and higher surcharges (1 July 2021) |
| Commercial Vehicle Emissions Scheme (CVES) | Introduced 1 April 2021 for light commercial vehicles |
| Motorcycle Standards | Tighter in-use standards from 6 April 2023; banned after 30 June 2028 |
| ICE Phase-Out | No new diesel car/taxi registrations from 2025; all new car/taxi registrations cleaner energy models by 2030 |
