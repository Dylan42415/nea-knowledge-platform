# Benzophenone-type UV filters


## Summary
Environmental contaminants including benzophenone-1 and benzophenone-3 evaluated for their toxic effects on algae and cyanobacteria.

## Key Data / Findings

| Substance | Organism | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Benzophenone-1 & 3 | Microcystis aeruginosa | Antioxidant responses |
| Benzophenone-3 | Chlamydomonas reinhardtii | Toxicity |
