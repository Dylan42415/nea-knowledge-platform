# Peatland restoration


## Summary
Environmental management efforts including re-wetting and re-vegetating peat-lands to increase moisture, potentially shifting fire dynamics from flaming to smoldering.

## Source Excerpt
> peatland restoration efforts, which includes re-wetting and re-vegetating peat-lands to replenish moisture content on- and under-ground, may be rendering more smoldering than flaming when fires occur.
 — soe_report.pdf, p. 49
