# World Health Organisation Water Quality Guidelines


## Summary
The World Health Organisation (WHO) guidelines for recreational water quality establish safety thresholds for public health protection, allowing primary contact activity when 95th percentile Enterococcus counts do not exceed 200 counts/100 ml.

## Key Data / Findings

| Parameter | Threshold Limit | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Enterococcus | ≤ 200 counts/100 ml (95th percentile) | Primary Contact Recreational Beach Activity |
