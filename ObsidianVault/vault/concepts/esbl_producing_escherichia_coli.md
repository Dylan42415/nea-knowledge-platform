# ESBL-Producing Escherichia coli


## Summary
Extended-spectrum β-lactamases (ESBL) resistant Escherichia coli is a multidrug-resistant bacterium monitored in Singapore's coastal and aquaculture environments. Between 2015 and 2019, local monitoring detected average concentrations of 10 CFU/100mL in marine waters.

## Key Data / Findings

| Location / Study Site | Concentration | Comparison Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Singapore Coastal & Aquaculture Sites (2015–2019) | 10 CFU/100mL | Local Baseline |
| Netherlands Recreational Coastal Waters (2010 Study) | 1 CFU/100mL | International Benchmark |
| Other Reported Regions | Detected | Coastal/recreational waters of Brazil, Norway, India |
