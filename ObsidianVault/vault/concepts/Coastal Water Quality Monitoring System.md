# Coastal Water Quality Monitoring System


## Summary
The Coastal Water Quality Monitoring System is operated by NEA along the Straits of Singapore and Straits of Johor. Commissioned in 2014, it includes 43 manual sampling points and 8 buoy-based real-time stations measuring 15 key parameters.

## Key Data / Findings

| Infrastructure Component | Quantity / Scope | Parameters |
|---|---|---|
| Manual Sampling Points | 43 points in Straits of Singapore & Johor | Physical, chemical, biological, microbiological parameters |
| Buoy-based Continuous Stations | 8 buoy stations (Commissioned 2014) | 15 key parameters (temperature, salinity, pH, DO, chlorophyll-a, turbidity) |
