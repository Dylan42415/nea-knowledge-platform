# Sulphates


## Summary
Sulphates represent one of the primary constituents of PM2.5 in Singapore. They are chemical compounds principally derived from regional sulphur dioxide (SO2) emissions generated by shipping activities and industrial oil refineries.

## Key Data / Findings

| Property | Details |
|---|---|
| Concentration Share | Consistently one of the two highest constituents of PM2.5 |
| Primary Origin | Shipping emissions, Petroleum refining |
