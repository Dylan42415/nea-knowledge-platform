# Transported Peat-Forest Smoke


## Summary
Transported peat-forest smoke significantly alters the physicochemical properties of fine particulate matter in Singapore. During the 2015 south-west monsoon, smoke persisted for around three months during an El Niño year, causing underground peat fires and heavy haze. Between 2011 and 2019, biomass burning smoke accounted for an average of ~30% of urban PM2.5 in Singapore.
