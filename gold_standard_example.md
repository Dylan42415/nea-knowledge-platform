# Pollutant Standards Index (PSI)

## Summary
The PSI is the index Singapore's National Environment Agency (NEA) uses to report
daily ambient air quality. It is computed from 24-hour concentrations of six
criteria pollutants and is paired with a health advisory for the public.

## Key Data / Findings

**Health Advisory Bands (24-hour PSI Descriptor):**

| PSI Range | Descriptor | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 0–50 | Good | Normal activities for all groups |
| 51–100 | Moderate | Normal activities for all groups |
| 101–200 | Unhealthy | Healthy persons: reduce prolonged/strenuous outdoor exertion. Elderly/pregnant/children: minimise prolonged/strenuous outdoor exertion. Persons with chronic lung/heart disease: avoid prolonged/strenuous outdoor exertion |
| 201–300 | Very Unhealthy | Healthy persons: avoid prolonged/strenuous outdoor exertion. Elderly/pregnant/children: minimise outdoor activity. Persons with chronic lung/heart disease: avoid outdoor activity |
| >300 | Hazardous | Healthy persons: minimise outdoor activity. Elderly/pregnant/children and persons with chronic lung/heart disease: avoid outdoor activity |

**Pollutants used in the 24-hour PSI computation:** PM10, PM2.5 (added 2014),
SO2, NO2, CO, O3.

**10-year trend, % of days in each band (2011–2020):**

| Year | Good | Moderate | Unhealthy | Very Unhealthy | Hazardous |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | 96 | 4 | – | – | – |
| 2012 | 93 | 7 | – | – | – |
| 2013 | 94 | 4 | 1 | 1 | – |
| 2014 | 83.5 | 11 | 5 | – | – |
| 2015 | 91.5 | 7.5 | 1 | 0.5 | – |
| 2016 | 90.5 | 9 | 0.5 | – | – |
| 2017 | 94 | 6 | – | – | – |
| 2018 | 86 | 11 | 3 | – | – |
| 2019 | 80 | 20 | – | – | – |
| 2020 | 80 | 20 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

*Note: air quality was affected by transboundary haze in 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016
and 2019. In 2014, PM2.5 was added to the PSI computation, which lowered the
"Good" percentage that year.*

**2020 performance:** Air quality was in the "Good" and "Moderate" ranges on
all days of 2020 — no Unhealthy, Very Unhealthy, or Hazardous days recorded.
The source attributes this to reduced economic/transport activity during
COVID-19 and relatively wet weather during the traditional dry season.

## Relationships
- **COMPUTED_FROM** → PM10, PM2.5, SO2, NO2, CO, O3 (six criteria pollutants,
  per source text)
- **BENCHMARKED_AGAINST** → WHO Air Quality Guidelines (2005) (source states
  NEA "strives towards achieving the WHO AQG (2005) in the long term")

## Source Excerpt
> "The PSI is an index to provide easily understandable information about
> daily levels of air quality. The levels of the six criteria pollutants are
> continuously monitored and their concentrations over a 24-hour period are
> used to compute the 24-hour PSI."
